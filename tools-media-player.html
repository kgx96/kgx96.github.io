Media Player

Screens that play YouTube, videos and web pages; others tune in to their channel.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Media Player places screens that play YouTube, YouTube Music and Spotify. With Internet set to Full (Utilities > Media & Radio) they also show any web page (Twitch, Vimeo, a site's own player) and play video / audio files (mp4, webm, mp3...); Restricted allows only the sites the server lists. Press E on a screen to open its control bar, paste a link and play it. Sound gets quieter as you walk away.

A screen owned by a Cinema Owner (or an admin) plays what they pick, and is a channel others tune in to. Every other screen, bought TVs too, is off at first. E turns it on and lets the player pick a channel.

You can place a TV model or a flat screen in one of five sizes. You can also turn a prop you spawned with a listed model into a screen.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Media Player.

Admins can use it, players who are given it in Settings > Player Access, and players in a job with the Cinema Owner ability. Volume, which sites may play, and how many screens each player may have are in Utilities > Media & Radio.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own screens (limit), from their own props.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a screen where you aim (model or flat size below)
Right click Make the aimed prop (a model from the list) one
Reload Remove the aimed one (yours; admins: any)

While you hold the tool, aiming at a screen shows its owner (or Server), "(kept for the map)" if kept, and what is on air.

Options

Option What it does Default
Screen The model picked below, or a flat screen: Small (48 units wide), Medium (96), Large (160), Huge (256) or Projector (448). The model picked below
Model icons Click a model to use it. Models of games you do not have are not shown. models/props/cs_office/tv_plasma.mdl
Scan map props (pick one the map uses) Admins only. Pick any prop model the map uses.
Keep it for this map (admins; nobody owns it, only admins control it) Admins only. The screen is saved for the map and has no owner. Off

How to use it

Put up a screen and play a video
  1. Set Screen to a flat size, for example Flat screen: Large (160 units wide).
  2. Aim at a wall and left-click. You get "Placed. E on it: the control bar."
  3. Press E on the screen, paste a link (YouTube, or what the server allows) and play it. Or click Browser, go to the video and click Play this here.
  4. E on a screen that plays something also gives you the cursor on it: click, scroll and type on the page, live for everyone (Utilities > Media & Radio). Settings in the control bar: picture, sound (range, loudness by distance) and who may use the page.
Use a TV model
  1. Set Screen to The model picked below and click a model icon.
  2. Left-click where the TV should go.
Turn a spawned prop into a screen
  1. Spawn a prop with one of the listed models.
  2. Aim at it and right-click. It becomes a screen in the same spot, owned by you (or kept for the map, if that box is ticked).
  3. A radio model becomes a radio instead ("That model makes a radio.").
Keep a screen for the map (admins)
  1. Tick Keep it for this map (admins; nobody owns it, only admins control it).
  2. Place the screen. You get "Placed (kept for this map)." It comes back after a restart.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_media 1 = media players (screens) work. 1 0-1
kgxat_media_jobs_only 1 = only Cinema Owner / DJ jobs (and admins) broadcast; 0 = every device's owner may play things. 1 0-1
kgxat_media_range How far away (units) screens show their picture and players hear them. 1500 300-6000
kgxat_media_max_user Screens one player may have. 1 0-20
kgxat_media_max_admin Screens one admin may have. 2 0-50
kgxat_media_max_super Screens one superadmin may have. 10 0-100

Tips and limits

  • Only a prop you spawned can become a screen (never another player's, a map, permanent or server-owned prop); admins can convert any.
  • At the limit you get "You have 1 screen already (the most allowed). Remove one first." Kept screens do not count.
  • Screens and radios have separate limits. Radios are placed with Building > Radio.
  • Only the owner and admins control a screen and can remove it with reload.
  • Admins may use any prop model. Cinema Owners and other players keep to the listed models.
  • Stores can sell TVs with the Electronics Store preset.
  • When kgxat_media is off, placing gives "Media players are turned off on this server."
