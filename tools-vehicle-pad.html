Vehicle Pad

Pads that give players a vehicle, free or for a price. Players press E on the pad.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

A vehicle pad hands out one vehicle from the spawn menu's Vehicles list. Players press E on the pad and choose Take it. The vehicle appears on the pad, facing the way you looked when you placed it.

A pad can be free or have a price, paid from the wallet. Each player can have one pad vehicle out at a time. It is removed when they take another one, leave the server, or leave it unused for the pad's despawn time.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Economy > Vehicle Pad.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it only if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access. Players can only change or remove their own pads.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Vehicles they may spawn, near them.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a vehicle pad (the vehicle faces where you look)
Right click Remove the aimed vehicle pad
Reload Update the aimed pad with the current settings
E + reload Open or close the aimed pad

Options

Option What it does Default
Vehicle: Shows the vehicle that is selected. Jeep
Search vehicles... Filters the list by name, key or category. empty
Vehicle list Columns Vehicle and Category. Click a row to select it. -
Price (0 = free) Price paid from the wallet, 0 to 100,000. 0
Remove when unused (min, 0 = never) Minutes an unused pad vehicle stays, 0 to 120. 10
Wait between vehicles (s) Seconds before the same player can take another vehicle from this pad, 0 to 600. 30
Reset to defaults Sets every option back to its default. -

Below the options is a list of every vehicle pad on this map, with the columns #, Vehicle and Distance. Double-click a row to go there. Right-click a row for Go there, Rename..., Turn on or Turn off, Remove the vehicle it gave out, Copy position and Remove.

How to use it

Place a free car pad
  1. Search for the vehicle and click it in the list.
  2. Leave Price (0 = free) at 0.
  3. Face the way the car should point, aim at flat ground and left-click.
Sell a vehicle
  1. Select the vehicle and set Price (0 = free).
  2. Left-click flat ground to place the pad.
  3. A player presses E on the pad and clicks Take it (price). The money comes out of their wallet.
Change a pad you already placed
  1. Select the new vehicle and set the price and times.
  2. Aim at the pad and press Reload.
Close a pad for a while
  1. Aim at the pad, hold E and press Reload.
  2. Do it again to open it. Closing a pad removes the vehicles it gave out.

What players see

Pressing E on a pad opens a small window with the vehicle and its price. The buttons are Take it (or Take it (price)) and Cancel. A player who already has a pad vehicle out also gets Put mine away, and taking a new one removes the old one.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_vehiclepads 1 = vehicle pads give out vehicles. Settings > Gameplay > Vehicle pads on. 1 0-1
kgxat_limit_vehiclepads Most vehicle pads one non-admin player can place (0 = not allowed). 1 0-32

Tips and limits

  • Aim at flat ground. A pad cannot be placed within 200 units of another pad.
  • The pad must be clear (no players, vehicles, NPCs or loose props) to give out a vehicle. The player's own old pad vehicle does not count.
  • With the economy off, every pad is free.
  • Admins do not wait for Wait between vehicles (s).
  • Jailed players cannot take a vehicle.
  • Right-click and Reload work on the aimed pad, or on the nearest pad within 160 units of where you aim.
  • Pads are saved per map with the other world data (vehiclepads_<map>.json). They cannot be moved with the physgun.
  • If the pad's vehicle is not installed on the server, the pad says so and gives nothing.
