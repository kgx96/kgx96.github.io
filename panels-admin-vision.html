Admin Vision

See players, NPCs, vehicles, props, weapons and entities through walls with outlines and labels, plus every zone, spawn point and spawner.

What it does

Admin Vision shows things through walls. Players get their name, rank, health, armor, distance and weapon. NPCs show health, faction and spawner; vehicles show their driver; props show their owner. Weapons lying around, toolbox entities and other entities can be shown too.

Each group can be turned on or off, with outlines and labels. Markers also draw every zone, spawn point, NPC spawner, item spawner and jail location on the map.

All of it is drawn on your own screen only. The settings are your own and nobody else sees them.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Admin Vision.

  • Toolbox admins can use it. Players can get it through Settings > Player Access with the Admin Vision tick.
  • C menu: Admin vision on / off.
  • Key: set one in Settings > Keybinds (Admin Vision: on/off (admins), recommended middle mouse button). The key only works for toolbox admins.
  • Console: kgxat_vision_toggle (bind a key to it, e.g. bind v kgxat_vision_toggle).

How to use it

Watch players through walls
  1. Open Utilities > Admin Vision and tick Admin Vision on.
  2. Leave Players ticked. Names, rank, HP, armor, distance in meters and the held weapon show above each player.
  3. Lower Range (units) if the screen gets crowded.
Find who owns a prop
  1. Tick Props and ragdolls and Labels on NPCs, vehicles, props and other entities.
  2. Look around: each prop shows its model name, distance and "owner (name)".
Check map setup
  1. Tick Show every zone, spawn point and NPC spawner.
  2. Walk the map. Zones, spawn points, NPC spawners, item spawners and jail locations are drawn without holding their tools.
Toggle it with a key
  1. Open Settings > Keybinds and set Admin Vision: on/off (admins).
  2. Press the key to switch Admin Vision on and off.

Settings and commands

All are client settings, saved for you.

kgxat_vision_labels 1
kgxat_vision_markers 1
kgxat_vision_players 1

kgxat_vision_vehicles 1
kgxat_vision_props 0
kgxat_vision_weapons 0
kgxat_vision_toolbox 1
kgxat_vision_other 0
kgxat_vision_key 0 (no key)
Command What it does
kgxat_vision_toggle Turns Admin Vision on or off.

Tips and limits

  • It only shows what the server is sending to you: things nearby or in view of the map. Far-away things may not appear.
  • Labels are shown for the nearest 80 entities. Outlines stop at 250 things.
  • The list of shown things updates 4 times a second.
  • Hidden (no-draw) entities and players who are dead are not shown.
  • The Admin Vision key is ignored while a menu is open.
