KGX Core (7DTD)

Bank payments & trade events

The framework every KGX 7 Days to Die mod runs on: one settings window, one look, one HUD. Version 1.2.0 lets KGX mods pay from the bank and react to trades.

  • KGX Settings window (F2): a page for every KGX mod with its server settings (admins), your personal settings and an on/off switch per mod
  • Mouse tooltips on every mod, setting and button; seven colour themes: KGX, Midnight, Forest, Desert, Steel, High Contrast and Custom (20-colour palette)
  • HUD toasts and a HUD tracker with one section per mod; top left or right, shrinks to a tab when nothing changes, F5 opens it
  • Multiplayer: a version check when a player joins switches off any KGX mod that does not match the server; messages are checked and rate limited; admin levels from serveradmin.xml
  • World and player saves with automatic backups (.bak1 to .bak3) and recovery from a damaged file
  • Console commands under kgx (kgx help, kgx mods, kgx diag); falls back to the game's tooltips when another mod replaces the HUD
  • New in 1.1.0: a shared payout event, so KGX Contract Board can pay Dukes into KGX Dukes Bank without either mod needing the other
  • New in 1.1.0: kgx <mod> help shows every command in full, for example kgx contracts refresh [player]
  • New in 1.2.0: bank payment events, so KGX Trade Post can take payments from KGX Dukes Bank and give Dukes back into it without either mod needing the other
  • New in 1.2.0: a trade event that KGX mods can listen to after every trade at KGX Trade Post

KGX Contract Board

Works with KGX Dukes Bank

A Contract Board you build yourself, with daily contracts for Dukes, XP and items. Version 1.0.1 can pay contract Dukes into KGX Dukes Bank.

  • Craft the Contract Board (10 wood, 10 nails, 5 paper) and press E: every player gets their own board with 5 offers and new offers every game day
  • Kill quotas (zombies, ferals, radiated, zombie dogs, cops, spiders), deliveries (wood, scrap iron, feathers, raw meat, cloth, bandages, oil, electrical parts), survive the blood moon or stay alive 12 to 48 hours, clear POIs of 1+ to 5+ skulls
  • Tiers from player level: harder contracts and 10% more reward per tier; up to 3 contracts at once
  • Party sharing: kills count for party members nearby; a POI clear counts for every player with a matching contract who was inside
  • Your contracts on the HUD tracker; mouse tooltips on every contract and button
  • Server settings (offers, contracts at once, refresh days, reward scale, POI clear %) and contracts.json for your own list; contracts that need an item or creature another mod removed are left out
  • New in 1.0.1: with KGX Dukes Bank installed, choose “Contract Dukes: into the bank” at a KGX ATM and contract Dukes go straight into your bank account (optional; Contract Board works without it)

KGX Dukes Bank

Pay with your bank

A craftable ATM with a bank account for every player. Version 1.1.0 lets you pay at KGX Trade Post straight from your account.

  • Craft the KGX ATM (10 forged iron, 4 mechanical parts, 4 electrical parts) and press E: your own account, and Dukes in it are safe when you die
  • Deposit an amount or everything you carry; withdraw up to 100,000 Dukes at a time
  • Send Dukes by name to any player who has played on this world, also when they are offline
  • Interest at the start of every game day: 1% by default, up to 250 Dukes a day, from a balance of 100 (server settings)
  • Party account: open one for your party; members deposit and withdraw, and the owner can close it and keep the Dukes
  • Works with KGX Contract Board: choose to have contract Dukes paid straight into your account
  • History of the last 50 lines per account, with a Clear history button that asks first; every deposit, withdrawal and transfer is logged on the server
  • Server settings in the KGX Settings window (F2) and admin commands under kgx bank
  • New in 1.1.0: works with KGX Trade Post - choose “Pay from: Bank” and buy orders, purchases and fees come out of your own account; unspent Dukes go back into it
  • New in 1.1.0: server setting payments to turn paying from the bank off
Full Dukes Bank roadmap

KGX Server Info

First release

KGX Wildlife

Deer herds, hunting packs and animal populations that remember how hard each biome has been hunted.
