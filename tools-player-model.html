Player Model

Change any player's model.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it
  3. Controls
  4. The panel
  5. How to use it
  6. Tips and limits

What it does

Player Model puts a player into any installed player model. Pick a model in the panel's grid, then click a player or yourself. Reload gives a player their own model back, the one they chose in their player model menu.

When you change a model, the skin and bodygroups are reset and the hands are updated to match. With Keep the model after respawning ticked, the player keeps it through death.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Cheats > Player Model.

Toolbox admins can use it. With a CAMI admin mod (ULX, SAM, sAdmin) that means the KGX Admin Toolbox privilege, default rank admin. Without one, admin and superadmin can use it. A superadmin can give it to non-admin players, or to one rank, in Settings > Player Access. The panel's two buttons need the same access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Themselves, player models, until they respawn.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Give the aimed player the selected model
Right click Give yourself the selected model
Reload Give the aimed player (or you) their own model back

The panel

At the top are a search box (Search models...) and a grid of model icons. The grid holds the same installed player models as the player model menu. Hover over an icon to see its name. Click an icon to select it; the selected one has a blue outline.

Option What it does Default
Apply the selected model to me Puts the selected model on you, without the tool gun.
Give me my own model back Gives you your own model back, without the tool gun.
Keep the model after respawning The model stays through death and respawn. On
Reset to defaults Selects models/player/kleiner.mdl and ticks keep again.

How to use it

Dress a player up
  1. Type part of a name in Search models..., for example "alyx".
  2. Click the icon you want.
  3. Aim at the player and left-click.
Change your own model quickly
  1. Select a model in the grid.
  2. Click Apply the selected model to me, or right-click with the tool.
  3. Click Give me my own model back to undo it.
Give a player their own model back
  1. Aim at the player and press reload. Aim at nothing to do it to yourself.
  2. Their model goes back to the one they picked themselves, and "keep" is cleared for them.

Tips and limits

  • Only installed models can be set. Otherwise you get "That model is not installed on the server".
  • If the gamemode or another addon puts the old model back, the tool applies yours again and tells you.
  • A model kept with this tool wins over a loadout model, a job model and a citizen or wardrobe look.
  • With keep off, the model lasts until the player respawns.
  • Works on players only. Rank immunity applies.
