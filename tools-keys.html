Keys

Lock and unlock any door, and set who owns a door and who has keys.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Keys is the admin side of the toolbox key system. It locks and unlocks any door, including map doors, and the vehicles players spawned. It can also freeze a door so it cannot open or close, or force a door open or closed even when locked.

For Door Maker doors, it opens the owner and key menu. There you set the owner and give or take keys. For property doors, it opens the property window.

The panel also holds the Keys settings, buttons to open or close every door at once, and saved lock states for all map doors.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Keys.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. The Settings (superadmins) checkboxes in the panel can only be changed by superadmins. Only superadmins lock or unlock server property.

Players carry the Keys weapon, and admins also Admin Keys (spawn menu: Weapons > ATB). Utilities > Player Manager: right-click a player for Give Keys, Give Admin Keys and Take their keys.

Admins only (v3.6.11.3): this tool sets up the server, so it cannot be given to players; it is greyed out in Settings > Player Access.

The owner and key menu

E + Reload on a Door Maker door opens a window titled with the door's owner.

  • Lock the door / Unlock the door.
  • A keys list. Right-click a name > Take the key back.
  • Give a key to... picks a player.
  • Give the door up (no owner), shown to the owner.
  • Admin: Make someone the owner... and No owner (clears keys and lock).

How to use it

Lock a map door
  1. Aim at the door. Make sure the HUD shows Lock / Unlock.
  2. Left-click to lock, right-click to unlock. The single door's state is saved for this map.
Stop a door from moving
  1. Press Reload until the mode is Freeze / Unfreeze.
  2. Left-click the door. It stays as it is. Right-click to unfreeze.
Change who owns a Door Maker door
  1. Aim at the door and press E + Reload.
  2. Click Make someone the owner... and pick a player.
  3. Use Give a key to... for anyone else who should open it.

Tips and limits

  • Lock / Unlock works on map doors, property doors, Door Maker doors and vehicles (not seats). Freeze and Open / Close work on doors only.
  • A single map door locked with this tool or Admin Keys is saved and wins over the all-doors setting. The three map door buttons clear those.
  • Map door locks are put back after cleanups and restarts. Doors of a bought property are left alone. Lockpicked doors are not saved.
  • A locked Door Maker door opens with E only for its owner, key holders and admins. Buttons and keypads still open it.
  • func_door also covers some lifts and gates. A locked one does not move.
  • Keys cannot be dropped or put in storage.
