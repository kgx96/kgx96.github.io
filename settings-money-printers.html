Money Printers

Server settings for money printers: print amount and speed, wear, upgrade tiers, the Repair Tool, the Printer Hacker and black market prices.

What it does

This page sets how every money printer on the server behaves. It covers how much a printer prints and how often, how fast it wears out, and what each upgrade tier adds.

It also sets the Repair Tool and Printer Hacker, and the black market prices. The Black Market Dealer job buys printers, upgrades and tools at those prices with !blackmarket.

Printers themselves are placed with Economy > Money Printer.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Money Printers.

Only superadmins can change these settings.

How to use it

Make printers earn more but break faster
  1. Raise Money per print, for example to 500.
  2. Raise Health lost per print (%), for example to 8.
  3. Click Apply. Running prints follow the new speed at once.
Make printers safe from damage
  1. Untick Printers lose health when dropped, hit, shot or blown up.
  2. Set Health lost per print (%) to 0 and click Apply. Printers now never wear out.
Set up the black market
  1. Add the Black Market Dealer job: Settings > Jobs > Add a preset job.
  2. Set the prices here and the dealer's share, then click Apply.
  3. Players with that job type !blackmarket to buy. Items appear in front of them, to sell or hand to other players.

Tips and limits

  • Printers also stop while the economy is off.
  • The Black Market Dealer store (Store Maker preset "Black Market Dealer") has its own item list and prices; edit them in the store editor.
  • The Money Printer item costs the printer price above in every store and preset. It is set when the price changes.
  • Spawn protection: a bought printer takes no damage, and only its buyer and admins can carry, move, hack or claim it until the time runs out. Its panel shows PROTECTED and the seconds left.
  • A player can set a printer to print less each time with its SETTINGS button. Smaller prints come faster, but it wears out sooner.
  • The printer price, most per player and spawn protection can also be set from the Money Printer tool (UPDATE SETTINGS).
