Citizen Looks

Choose the models citizens wear and let new players pick their own look and player and weapon colors.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Citizen Looks gives citizens (and the Unemployed in RP mode) one of a list of citizen models. Players who did not pick get a random model each time they join, or always the same one for their SteamID.

New players can pick their own look when they join, or right after accepting the rules. They choose Male, Female or Non-binary (any), then a model or Random, plus a player and weapon color. !look opens the window again later.

Looks only apply while three things are on: citizens (Settings > Gameplay > Citizens), Jobs set their player model (Settings > Jobs), and Citizen looks on on this page.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Citizen Looks.

Only superadmins can change these settings and the model list. The model list is filled for toolbox admins.

Players open their own look window with !look, !citizen, !mylook, the console command kgxat_look, or (for players) the C menu's ATB window, Me tab > My look (citizen model and colors).

How to use it

Set up citizen looks
  1. In Settings > Gameplay, set Citizens to In RP mode or In every mode.
  2. In Settings > Jobs, make sure Jobs set their player model is ticked.
  3. Open Settings > Citizen Looks and tick Citizen looks on (citizens wear the models below).
  4. Pick how players who did not choose get a model in Citizens who did not pick.
Add a model to the list
  1. Type a path in Add a model (path or player model name), or choose one in ...or pick a player model.
  2. Set Group of the new model to Male or Female, or leave it to read the file name.
  3. Click Add it. The model must be on the server.
Pick your own look as a player
  1. Type !look in chat.
  2. Click Male, Female or Non-binary (any) to filter the models.
  3. Click a model, or click Random for a random one of that group.
  4. Set Player color and Weapon color, then click Save (This is me the first time).

kgxat_block_playermodel Hide the context menu's Player Model button 1 0-1
!look, !citizen, !mylook, kgxat_look Open your look window

Tips and limits

  • At most 128 models in the list. Models the server does not have are skipped.
  • The list is saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/citizen_looks.json. Players' picks and colors are in citizen_choices.json.
  • Color picks apply to every player, not only citizens.
  • The newest look wins: a model picked with !look replaces a Wardrobe look, and a Wardrobe look replaces the !look model (colors stay).
  • With Citizens may change their look any time off and colors on, !look only shows the color pickers.
