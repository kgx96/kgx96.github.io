Player Effects

Give players a trail, a glow or sparkles.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

On this page

  2. Where to find it
  3. Controls

What it does

Player Effects decorates players with one of three effects in the color you pick:

  • Trail: a sprite trail that follows the player, in one of eight looks.
  • Glow: a colored outline around the player.
  • Sparkles: small glowing particles drifting around the player.

Effects stack, one of each kind. Giving a player a trail again replaces their old trail. Effects stay through respawns until you remove them.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Fun > Player Effects.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Themselves (others with kgxat_grant_others).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Give the chosen effect to the player you aim at
Right click Give the chosen effect to yourself
Reload Remove all effects from the player you aim at (or yourself)

Options

How to use it

Give a player a trail
  1. Set Effect to Trail (follows the player).
  2. Pick a Color and a Trail look, and set the length and width.
  3. Aim at the player and left-click.
Make yourself glow and sparkle
  1. Set Effect to Glow (colored outline) and right-click.
  2. Set Effect to Sparkles, change the color if you want, and right-click again. You now have both.
Remove effects
  1. Aim at the player and press reload. All their effects are removed.
  2. Press reload while aiming at nothing to remove your own.

Tips and limits

  • Works on players only.
  • Cloaked players do not show their effects.
  • Sparkles are only drawn for viewers within about 2000 units.
  • Rank immunity applies: you cannot change effects on players ranked above you.
  • Each change is written to the action log.
