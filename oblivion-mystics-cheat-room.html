v1.1
Out now/ 01

Cheat Menu anywhere & a companion

The cheat room, everywhere: a portable Cheat Menu, a welcome page, Demora the companion, refilling chests and furnished rooms. Vanilla Oblivion GOTY, no OBSE needed.

  • Requires Oblivion GOTY or Deluxe (Shivering Isles content is used). OBSE is not needed
  • *Cheats: Cheat Menu: a power that opens the Cheat Menu anywhere. PLAYER, STATS & SKILLS, ITEMS, MAGIC, CHARACTER, WORLD, TELEPORT, TOGGLES and COMPANION pages
  • Toggles: god mode, unlimited carry, super speed, water breathing and walking, night eye, invisibility and detect life
  • Teleport to the Cheat Room and every city from the menu
  • A welcome page once per save: get the Cheat Menu power or fast travel to the Cheat Room. Traveling during the prologue completes it like leaving the sewers, with the edit-character screen
  • Demora, the Cheat Room companion: follows, waits, heals, barters, repairs and opens Storage 1–6 from anywhere. Can't die and never turns on you. Summon him with *Cheats: Summon Demora
  • Every "All ..." chest refills itself when you close it; Storage Container 1–6 keep your own items
  • The rooms: the All Items room and the Bed Chambers furnished, invisible walls around the Doomstone platform
  • Fixed: no more edits to vanilla lights, the Arcane University enchanting altar, the Anvil Fighters Guild or the Abandoned Mine; Market District objects are disabled instead of deleted
  • Built on v1.0 (Aug 2023): the room with every item, the cheat buttons and the custom spells

02 / About the project

Made for
Oblivion.

Mystic's Cheat Room

A cheat room in the Imperial City Market District: activate the rock in front of the statue, or take the welcome page's fast travel. Inside: chests with every item in the game that refill when you close them, cheat buttons and altars, Storage Container 1–6, the Bed Chambers and Demora, the Cheat Room companion. The *Cheats: Cheat Menu power brings the cheats with you anywhere.

View the mod on Nexus Mods

Getting started

Needs The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion GOTY or Deluxe (Shivering Isles content is used); no OBSE. Download the main file from the Files tab on Nexus Mods, put Mystic's Cheat Room.esp in Oblivion\Data and enable it under Data Files. Save first. After you create your character, a welcome page offers the Cheat Menu power or a fast travel to the room.
