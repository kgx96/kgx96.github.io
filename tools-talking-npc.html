Talking NPC

Characters that talk to players: pages of text and choices that can warp, give items, money or a job.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Talking NPC places a character that players talk to with E. It shows pages of text with up to six choices each. Choices can show another page, end the talk, warp the player, give a weapon, money, health or armor, give a job, sell a Property License, open the wardrobe, or fire a logic node.

Each NPC has its own look, voice, talk range and rules for who may talk to it. It can stand still, wander around its spot, or be a real NPC that holds a weapon and follows AI orders.

Talking NPCs are saved for this map and come back after cleanups and restarts. Presets let you reuse a finished NPC on every map.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Talking NPC.

Toolbox admins can use it. Superadmins can also give it to players in Settings > Player Access. Players then edit only their own NPCs and can load presets, but cannot save choices that give things.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own talking NPCs: no rewards, weapons or private warps.

The editor

Right-click an NPC to open the editor. The top row has Name, Model and Reward cooldown (s) (default 300). The bottom row has Save as preset..., Load a preset..., Preview (not saved) and Save.

How to use it

Make a healer NPC
  1. Left-click the floor to place an NPC, then right-click it.
  2. On Pages, write page 1: Hello {name}. Need patching up?
  3. Click Add a choice: Button text Heal me, action Heal (HP), value 50.
  4. Add a choice Bye. with End the talk and click Save.
  5. Press E on the NPC to test it.
Send players to a place
  1. Make a warp point in Functions > Warp Points.
  2. Add a choice with Warp the player (warp point name).
  3. Click ... and pick the warp under Warp points, then Save.
Reuse an NPC as a preset
  1. Finish an NPC in the editor and click Save as preset....
  2. Enter a name. An existing name is replaced.
  3. Select it in Preset and left-click to place copies, or hold E and right-click an NPC to apply it.
Make a guard who talks
  1. In Settings, set Body to Real NPC (weapons, patrols, AI Brain, Commander, tags).
  2. Use a citizen, combine or story model, then pick a Weapon and Attitude to players.
  3. Click Save. Players still press E to talk.

Settings and commands

Tips and limits

  • Saved per map in talknpcs_<map>.json. Presets are saved for every map in talkpresets.json.
  • At most 64 talking NPCs per map and 64 presets.
  • Aim at the floor to place one. It faces you.
  • Only admins can save or delete presets.
  • Choices that give weapons, money, heal, armor or a license are turned into End the talk when a player saves them. Players also cannot set a weapon, a tag, health or hostility.
  • Player models (models/player/...) have no NPC animations. As a real NPC they stand in a T pose; the editor warns you.
