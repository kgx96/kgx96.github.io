Create Chairs

Make seats on anything, draw rows of seats, seat talking NPCs.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it
  3. Controls
  4. Options

What it does

Create Chairs makes seats players can sit on. Left click puts an invisible seat where you aim, facing you. On a prop, the seat moves with the prop.

Right click twice draws a row of seats along a bench, sofa or ledge. The seats are spread evenly between the two points and face the side you stand on.

The tool can also seat talking NPCs, and its panel lists every chair on the server, including chairs players bought.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Create Chairs.

Admins can use it, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access. The Server chairs checkbox is shown to admins only. Players put seats on the ground or on their own props, up to their limit (Settings > Limits, "Seats", 10), and remove or move only the seats they made and their own talking NPCs.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): On the ground or their own props (seats limit; seats on the ground last until they leave); their own seats only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click A seat here (facing you); on a seat with a talking NPC picked: the NPC sits there
Right click Draw a row of seats: first click one end, then the other
Reload On a seat: remove it. On a talking NPC: pick it
E + Reload On a prop: remove all its seats

While the tool is out, a blue see-through seat shows where the next seat goes and which way it faces. Existing seats show as blue boxes.

Options

Option What it does Default
Height Moves the seat up or down. Slider -40 to 40. 0
Forward Moves the seat forward or back. Slider -40 to 40. 0
Right Moves the seat sideways (single seats only). Slider -40 to 40. 0
Turn (degrees) Turns the seat. Slider -180 to 180. 0
Seat spacing in a row One seat every this many units in a row. Slider 16 to 80. 28
Server chairs (admins: belong to the server; Alt + E on one for who may sit) New seats belong to the server. Admins press Alt + E on a seat to choose who may sit. Off
Talking NPCs
Option What it does Default
NPC feet below the seat How far below the seat the NPC's feet go. Slider 0 to 40. 18
Chairs on the server

The list shows every seat made with this tool and every chair players bought. Columns: Kind, On / chair, Kept / owner and Sitting.

Control What it does
Double-click a row Go there.
Right-click a row Go there or Remove.
Refresh the list Reloads the list.
Remove the selected chair Removes the selected seat or chair.
Remove all bought chairs Removes every chair players bought, after you confirm. Seats made with this tool stay.

How to use it

Make a single seat on a prop
  1. Stand where a person sitting there should face you.
  2. Aim at the prop. Adjust Height, Forward, Right and Turn until the blue preview is right.
  3. Left-click. You get "Seat made".
Fill a bench with seats
  1. Stand on the side the seats should face.
  2. Right-click one end of the bench. You get "First end set."
  3. Right-click the other end. You get "N seats made along the row."
  4. If the seats are too high or low, remove them with E + reload on the bench, change Height, and draw again.
Seat a talking NPC
  1. Make a seat with left click.
  2. Aim at the talking NPC and press reload. You get "Talking NPC picked."
  3. Left-click the seat. The NPC sits there, facing the seat's way.

Tips and limits

  • Seats on the world, map props and permanent props are kept for this map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/seats_<map>.json.
  • Seats on spawned props last until the prop is removed. Make the prop permanent to keep them.
  • Seats a player (not an admin) puts on the ground are not saved for the map: they last until the player removes them or leaves.
  • A row has at most 24 seats. A longer row is refused; raise Seat spacing in a row.
  • The first end of a row expires after 60 seconds.
  • Seats in a row attach to the prop you clicked first. If that was the world, they are world seats.
  • A seated talking NPC stays put, does not walk around and does not turn to face players.
  • The NPC's model needs a sitting animation (citizens, player models). Otherwise you get an error.
  • Left click on a player or an NPC does nothing.
  • Players buy chairs in the F3 hub (Chairs tab).
