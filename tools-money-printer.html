Money Printer

Make money printers and upgrades, set the printer that is sold, and look after every printer.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

The Money Printer tool makes money printers. Place a new one on the ground, or left-click a prop that uses one of the printer models to turn it into a printer. A printer prints cash at a set speed while it is on, and wears down as it prints.

The same tool spawns upgrade modules (tier 1 to 3) or installs them straight into a printer. It also sets the main printer: the model and panel side that stores and the black market sell.

Players use a printer with E. Each printer has a panel with three buttons: ON/OFF, UPGRADE and SETTINGS. E anywhere else on the printer picks it up and carries it.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Economy > Money Printer.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it only if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access. That tick also opens the printer debug window to them (break, repair or remove any printer, set the main printer). Players place printers for themselves only, up to their limit (Settings > Limits, "Money printers placed with the tool", 2), turn only their own props into printers, and change or remove only their own. Upgrades and the main printer are for admins.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own printers (limit); no upgrades.

How to use it

Place a printer for a player
  1. Under Left-click, pick "Place a printer".
  2. Pick a Printer model and a panel side.
  3. Pick the player under Owner of new printers, or leave (me).
  4. Left-click the ground. The printer faces you; cash comes out of that side.
  5. Press E on its ON/OFF button to start it.
Turn a prop into a printer
  1. Spawn one of the printer models (the ATB spawn menu tab has them under Props > Money printer props).
  2. With "Place a printer" selected, left-click the prop.
  3. It becomes a printer in the same spot. The prop's owner owns it; with no owner, the tool's owner setting is used.
Upgrade a printer
  1. Under Left-click, pick "Upgrade module" and choose a Tier.
  2. Left-click a printer to install it at once, or click anywhere else to spawn the module.
  3. Players install a spawned module by putting it next to the printer and pressing its UPGRADE button.
Change the printer that stores sell
  1. Select the model and side in the Printer section.
  2. Click UPDATE SETTINGS. Or select "SET PRINTER" under Left-click and left-click a printer.
  3. UPDATE SETTINGS also changes printers in the world (if ticked), puts the price on every store's Money Printer item on this map and on the store presets, and refreshes open black market windows.

Tips and limits

  • A printer holds at most 3 upgrade modules.
  • Printing, drops, hits and shots wear a printer down. Below 30% it smokes; at 0% it stops until it is repaired with the Repair Tool.
  • While something blocks the front, the finished print waits.
  • A printer without an owner belongs to the first player who uses it.
  • Printers cannot go in a backpack or storage.
  • Z undoes printers and modules you made.
  • Spawn protection: a bought printer takes no damage, and only its buyer and admins can carry, move, hack or claim it until the time runs out.
  • Stores sell the main printer with the item type Money Printer. The Black Market Dealer store preset has one. The Black Market Dealer job buys printers with !blackmarket.
  • In the debug window, double-click a row to go there. Right-click a row for Go there, Turn on or Turn off, Print now, Repair fully, Set health..., Break it, Install upgrade, Remove all upgrades, Money per print, Panel side, Owner and Remove.
  • Print speed, money, wear and prices are in Settings > Money Printers.
