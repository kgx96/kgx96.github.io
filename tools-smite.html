Smite

Strike players, NPCs, props or the ground with lightning.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Smite calls down a lightning bolt with thunder, sparks and a scorch mark. Everyone sees the bolt.

It damages the target you aim at, plus anything inside the splash radius around the strike point. It can also set victims on fire, or kill them outright.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Fun > Smite.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): No "always kill" (others with kgxat_grant_others).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Strike what you aim at (or the ground)
Right click Strike the ground only (effect + splash)

While you hold the tool, a red sphere and a yellow ground ring show the splash radius at your crosshair.

Options

Option What it does Default Range
Damage Shock damage to each victim 100 0-1000
Splash radius (0 = target only) Also hits players, NPCs and anything with physics within this distance 64 0-1024
Set on fire (s, 0 = off) Sets each victim alight for this many seconds 5 0-60
Always kill (ignores damage) Kills players; other victims take enough damage to die or break Off -
Never hit myself You are never hit by your own strike On -
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to the defaults - -

How to use it

Smite one player
  1. Set Splash radius to 0 so only the target is hit.
  2. Aim at the player and left-click.
Strike an area
  1. Set Splash radius to the area you want, for example 300.
  2. Aim at the ground and right-click. Everything in the radius is hit.
Kill with lightning
  1. Tick Always kill (ignores damage).
  2. Left-click the target.

Settings and commands

These caps limit a limited player's Smite. Set them in Settings > Limits (superadmins only).

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_cap_smite_damage Highest damage a limited player gets 100 0-1000
kgxat_cap_area_radius Largest splash radius a limited player gets (shared with other area tools) 512 0-16384

Limited players are players given the tool, and admins when kgxat_limits_admins is 1. Superadmins are never limited.

Tips and limits

  • Indoors the bolt starts at the ceiling.
  • Left-clicking the world strikes the ground there, the same as right-click.
  • Right-click strikes the aim point even when you aim at an entity; the entity is only hit if it is in the splash radius.
  • Dead players are skipped. Players ranked above you are skipped when rank immunity is on, and left-click refuses them.
  • The splash never hits the toolbox's own things (pads, buttons, keypads, cameras), attached (parented) entities or held weapons.
  • Every strike is written to the action log, with the number of victims for ground strikes.
