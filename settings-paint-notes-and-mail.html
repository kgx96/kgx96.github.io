Paint, Notes & Mail

Server settings for the Spray Paint and Sponge, notes players stick on walls, and the player mail system.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

This page sets how the Spray Paint, Sponge and Notepad weapons work, and turns mail on or off. These are player tools found in the spawn menu under Weapons > ATB.

Spray Paint lets players paint on the map's walls and floors, and on doors if you allow it. Paint on a door moves with it. The Sponge rubs paint away again. The Notepad writes a note and sticks it on any surface for others to read.

Mail holds notes and messages from other players, also when they were away, and money mailed by store sales. Notes from mail can be pinned on a surface again.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Paint, Notes & Mail.

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings. The Remove all spray paint on the map button works for toolbox admins.

Related places:

  • Spray Paint, Sponge and Notepad: spawn menu > Weapons > ATB.
  • Admin notes: World > Notes.
  • Mail: !mail, F3 hub > Mail, or the backpack window.

How to use it

Limit paint on a busy server
  1. Lower Paint dots one player may have (oldest go first) and Paint dots on the whole map.
  2. Set Seconds paint stays above 0 to make paint fade on its own.
  3. Click Apply the numbers above.
Leave a note as a player
  1. Take the Notepad from Weapons > ATB.
  2. Right-click to write or edit the note.
  3. Left-click a surface to place it.
Clean up paint
  1. Players: take the Sponge and hold left click over paint. Right click removes a whole stroke.
  2. Admins: click Remove all spray paint on the map, or run kgxat_paint_clear <SteamID> for one player's paint.

Tips and limits

  • Paint never goes on props, players or NPCs. It goes on doors only with Paint sticks to doors too ticked.
  • When a player or the map reaches its paint limit, the oldest dots go first.
  • Changing the note look updates notes already up. A Notepad in someone's hands changes the next time it is given out.
  • A note look the server does not have is shown as the paper.
  • A note holds up to 2000 characters of text and a 48 character title.
