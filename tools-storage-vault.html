Storage Vault

Server vaults: everyone opens their own personal vault at them.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Storage Vault sets up server vaults. Everyone who presses E on a server vault opens their own personal vault. Only they see their things in it, and it is kept for every map.

Nobody can lockpick, move or break a server vault. You can place a new one, or turn any prop, entity or NPC into one.

Players can also buy their own vault in a store (item type "Storage Vault"). The panel holds the settings for both kinds.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Storage Vault.

Superadmins only. Other admins, and players given the tool, get "Only superadmins set up server vaults." The settings in the panel are also superadmin only.

Admins only (v3.6.11.3): this tool sets up the server, so it cannot be given to players; it is greyed out in Settings > Player Access.

How to use it

Place a bank vault
  1. Click a model in the panel, such as a safe or locker.
  2. Aim at the floor and left-click. The vault stands on the spot, facing you.
  3. Players press E on it to open their personal vault.
Turn a map prop into a vault
  1. Aim at a safe or locker that is part of the map.
  2. Right-click. It stays a map prop and becomes a server vault, kept for this map.
Take a vault away
  1. Aim at the vault and press reload.
  2. A server vault stops being a vault. The things in it stay in everyone's personal vault.
  3. On a player's bought vault, it is removed. They get it back with !vault, with its contents.
Let players buy their own vault
  1. Make sure Players may buy their own vault is ticked.
  2. Add a store item of type "Storage Vault" (Economy > Store Maker).
  3. A bought vault goes into the buyer's Pockets. They take it out where they like (not in a spawn zone); Alt + E on it picks its style.
  4. Leave Picking a placed vault back into the Pockets on Only when it is empty. A vault's things are saved for its owner, not inside the vault, so a vault that goes back into the Pockets full is a stash the player carries everywhere. With Never it stays where it was first put down.
Get rid of a bought vault (players)
  1. Take everything out of the vault.
  2. Alt + E on it and click Throw this vault away, then Throw it away. Or type !vault remove (also works while it is in the Pockets).
  3. It is gone from the world and the Pockets, and !vault or joining no longer hands it out. Buying one again gives a new one.

Settings and commands

Setting or command What it does Default Range
kgxat_vault 1 = Storage Vaults work (server vaults and bought ones). 1 0 to 1

!vault Chat. Puts your bought vault back in your pockets after a restart or cleanup (not while Storage Vaults are off).
!vault remove Chat. Throws your empty bought vault away (from the world or the Pockets).

Tips and limits

  • Personal vaults are kept for every map in vaults.json.
  • Spawned props made vaults become a vault of their own. Map props and permanent props are kept for the map. Other things stay a vault only until a restart.
  • Each player can own one bought vault. With no room in their Pockets, the sale fails.
  • Only the owner puts things in a bought vault. Others open it only with the Lockpick, and the owner is told.
  • A bought vault stays in the pockets on death. After a restart or cleanup, !vault (also on joining) puts it back. While Storage Vaults are off nothing is handed out.
  • Trying to pick a vault up too early or full says why: "You can pick your vault up again in ...", "Empty your vault first: only an empty vault goes back into your Pockets." or "Your vault stays where you put it."
