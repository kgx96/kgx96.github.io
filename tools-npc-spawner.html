NPC Spawner

Place markers that keep spawning any NPC: how many, how often, weapon and faction. Saved for this map.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

An NPC spawner is a marker that keeps up to a set number of NPCs alive near it. When one dies, a new one is made after the set number of seconds. It can also spawn the whole group at once as waves.

You choose any NPC from the spawn menu, addons included, or any npc_ class. You also set its weapon, faction (friendly, neutral or hostile to players), health and an optional NPC tag.

Spawners are saved per map. After a map cleanup they refill from the start.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > NPC Spawner.

Admins can use the tool, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access. Players can only remove, update or switch their own spawners, and cannot use admin-only NPCs. Players are limited by kgxat_limit_spawners (Settings > Limits). The server switches in the panel are for superadmins.

Admins only (v3.6.11.3): this tool sets up the server, so it cannot be given to players; it is greyed out in Settings > Player Access.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a spawner where you aim (the NPCs face you)
Right click Remove the aimed spawner and its NPCs
Reload Update the aimed spawner with the current settings
E + Reload Turn the aimed spawner on or off

Right click and reload act on the nearest spawner within 128 units of where you aim. Updating a spawner starts it over.

A tag makes the NPCs walk the patrol route or guard the post with that tag, and use the tag's profile (AI > Patrol & Guard, AI > NPC AI > NPC tags). The faction is also used by forcefield zones (World > Zones) to decide who may enter.

NPC spawners on this map

A list of every spawner with the columns #, Spawner and Distance. Double-click a row to go there. Right-click a row for:

  • Go there
  • Rename...
  • Turn on / Turn off
  • Spawn now (start over)
  • Remove its NPCs
  • Copy position
  • Remove

Refresh reloads the list. Remove all NPC spawners on this map... removes every spawner after a confirmation. It cannot be undone.

How to use it

Guard an area with hostile soldiers
  1. Search "combine" and pick a soldier.
  2. Set Faction to Hostile to players and How many alive at once to 4.
  3. Set Seconds between spawns to 30.
  4. Aim at the floor and left-click. The first NPC appears after about a second.
Run waves only when players are near
  1. Pick an NPC and tick Spawn the whole group at once (waves).
  2. Set Only with a player within to, for example, 1500.
  3. Place the spawner. It refills the whole group only while a player is in range.
Change or pause a spawner
  1. Change the options, aim at the spawner and press reload.
  2. To pause it, hold E and press reload. Do it again to turn it back on.
  3. To clear its NPCs without removing it, right-click its row and pick Remove its NPCs.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_spawners NPC spawners spawn NPCs. 1 0-1
kgxat_spawner_max Most NPCs alive from all NPC spawners together. 60 1-300
kgxat_limit_spawners Max NPC spawners for non-admin players (0 = not allowed). 2 0-64

Tips and limits

  • Spawners are saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/spawners_<map>.json.
  • The first NPC comes right away. After that, the timer starts once the spawner is no longer full.
  • Stop after this many in total counts since the map started. Spawn now (start over) resets the count.
  • Removing a spawner also removes the NPCs it made.
  • If an NPC cannot be spawned (for example, not installed), the server log notes it once and the spawner retries after at least 30 seconds.
  • Logic nodes can turn NPC spawners on or off or make them spawn now.
