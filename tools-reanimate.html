Reanimate

Bring dead NPCs back: they follow you and take orders.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Reanimate brings a dead NPC back where it died. It rises with the same kind, model, skin and weapon, likes you and follows you. It is named "Reanimated ..." and looks pale green by default.

Your reanimated NPCs defend you when a player or NPC attacks you. They are removed when you leave the server.

A command wheel gives them orders: follow, stay, go somewhere, attack, guard a spot, wander, come to you, or release.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > AI > Reanimate.

Admins can use it. Other players can use it only if they are given the tool in Settings > Player Access. The server settings in the panel can be changed by superadmins only.

You can also bind a key to open the command wheel at any time you have reanimated NPCs: in the tool panel or in Settings > Keybinds.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own reanimated NPCs (they drop no weapons); attack: never the server's NPCs, players only with kgxat_grant_others.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Reanimate the corpse you aim at (or where it died)
Right click Open the command wheel. Aim first: where to go, or whom to attack.
Reload All of them follow you

The command wheel

Order What it does
Follow me They follow you
Stay here They hold where they stand
Go there They run to where you aimed, spread out
Attack that They attack the player or NPC you aimed at
Guard there They guard the spot you aimed at, within 300 units
Wander They wander around
Come here They run to you
Release (they die) They die

Move the mouse toward an order and left click it. Right click closes the wheel. If you aimed at one of your own NPCs when you opened the wheel, the order goes to that one only (except Attack that).

How to use it

Raise a fallen soldier
  1. Kill an NPC, or find one that died recently.
  2. Aim at its corpse, or where it died, and left click.
  3. It rises and follows you. The HUD shows how many you have and how long corpses stay usable.
Send your NPCs to attack
  1. Aim at the target player or NPC.
  2. Right click (or press your wheel key) to open the wheel.
  3. Click Attack that.
Hold a doorway
  1. Aim at the doorway.
  2. Open the wheel and click Guard there.

Tips and limits

  • The corpse must be within 160 units of where you aim, and must have died within the reanimate window.
  • Deaths are only remembered while Reanimate is on. The server remembers the last 64 deaths.
  • Once you have the most allowed, you must release some before raising more.
  • Reanimated NPCs like each other. Other players are ignored unless kgxat_reanimate_hostile is on.
  • Attack that needs a player or NPC under your aim that is not you or one of yours.
  • Each reanimation is written to the server log.
