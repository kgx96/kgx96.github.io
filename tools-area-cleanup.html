Area Cleanup

Remove props, ragdolls, NPCs and dropped weapons in an area.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Area Cleanup removes clutter in one spot without cleaning up the whole map. Left-click removes the entity types you ticked inside the sphere at your crosshair. Players and held weapons are never removed.

Right-click works on the whole map. It removes NPC corpses and clears decals, such as blood and bullet holes, for everyone. Ragdolls spawned by players are kept.

A red sphere and a yellow ground ring at your crosshair show the area. Their size matches the Radius slider.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Area Cleanup.

Admins only by default. A superadmin can give it to all players or to one rank in Settings > Player Access, or add it to a Tool License (Settings > Licenses).

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own things only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left Remove the ticked entity types inside the red sphere.
Right Clear NPC corpses and decals on the whole map.

Options

Option What it does Default
Radius Size of the sphere, 16 to 8192 units. 512
Props Physics props (prop_physics variants) and effects (prop_effect). On
Ragdolls Ragdolls (prop_ragdoll). On
NPCs / NextBots NPCs and NextBots. Off
Weapons on the ground Weapons nobody holds. On
Other player-spawned entities Any other entity that has a player owner. Off
Include map-placed entities (careful) Also removes matching entities that are part of the map. Off
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to its default.

How to use it

Clear a messy build area
  1. Tick Props and Ragdolls. Untick the rest.
  2. Set Radius so the sphere covers the mess.
  3. Aim at the middle of the area and left-click.
  4. The tool reports how many entities it cleared.
Remove leftover NPCs after an event
  1. Tick NPCs / NextBots and Weapons on the ground.
  2. Untick Props and Ragdolls if you want to keep them.
  3. Left-click the area.
Clean up corpses and blood after a fight
  1. Aim anywhere.
  2. Right-click. Server corpses with no owner are removed, decals are cleared, and client-side corpses go too.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_cap_area_radius Largest radius a limited player can use (Settings > Limits). Admins are not capped unless kgxat_limits_admins is on; superadmins never are. 512 0-16384

Tips and limits

  • Players given this tool through Player Access clear only their own things: never map, permanent or server-owned entities or anything the Admin Remover protects; map does nothing for them.
  • There is no undo. Check your ticks before you click.
  • Weapons held by players or NPCs, and anything parented to another entity, are skipped. They go with their holder.
  • Other player-spawned entities only takes things with a player owner, so the gamemode's own entities are never touched.
  • Map-placed entities are skipped unless Include map-placed entities (careful) is ticked.
  • With built-in prop protection on (kgxat_pp), non-admin players only remove what they may touch. Admins remove anything that matches.
