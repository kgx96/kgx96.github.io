7 DAYS TO DIE MODS

7 Days to Die tutorials.Learn the KGX mods.

Step-by-step guides for every KGX mod for 7 Days to Die, for players and for server owners.

Start here

All KGX mods need KGX Core (7DTD) and 7 Days to Die V 3.4 (b10) on PC, with EasyAntiCheat off. In multiplayer every KGX mod goes on the server and on every player's game. Begin with installing KGX Core and KGX mods.
