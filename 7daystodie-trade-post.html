v1.0.0
Out now/ 01

First release

A craftable Trade Post where players buy and sell with each other, also when the other player is offline.

  • Craft the KGX Trade Post (8 forged iron, 2 mechanical parts, 10 paper) and press E
  • Buy orders with your Dukes held up front, filled by other players a part at a time; listings of backpack items kept exactly as they are (quality, mods, durability)
  • Instant trades: a listing sells at once to a buy order paying its price or more, and a buy order buys at once from listings at its price or less
  • Quality buy orders (for example a pistol of quality 4 or better), a price guide, price changes without cancelling, and private offers for one player
  • Collect box for bought items, unsold items and unspent Dukes; orders end after 7 game days by default; a toast when you join tells you what happened while you were away
  • Watch items, filter by category and sort by price or name
  • For server owners: server orders that buy or sell for Dukes (limited or unlimited), blocked items, a sale fee and a listing fee, local markets, and a trade log with kgx trade history
  • Only items players can get in normal play can be ordered; the Trade Post Item Catalog on this site lists them with their internal names
  • Works with KGX Dukes Bank: pay from your bank account, and unspent Dukes go back into it

02 / About the project

Made for
7 Days to Die traders.

KGX Trade Post

A Trade Post you craft and place yourself. Post what you want to buy with your Dukes held up front, or list items from your backpack exactly as they are, quality and mods included. Other players fill your orders and buy your listings while you are away, and whatever you are owed waits in your Collect box. Prices that meet trade at once, a price guide shows what items go for, and server owners get server orders, blocked items, fees, local markets and a trade log. With KGX Dukes Bank you can pay straight from your bank account.

KGX Trade Post on Nexus Mods

Getting started

Needs KGX Core (7DTD) 1.2.0 or newer and 7 Days to Die V 3.4 (b10) on PC with EasyAntiCheat off. Extract the zip into the game's Mods folder so you have Mods\KGX_TradePost next to Mods\0_KGX_Core; in multiplayer install both on the server and on every player's game. Craft the KGX Trade Post in your backpack (8 forged iron, 2 mechanical parts, 10 paper), place it and press E. Type kgx trade help in the console (F1) for the commands. Optional: KGX Dukes Bank 1.1.0 to pay from your bank account.
