Third Person

Toggle an over-the-shoulder third person camera with a key, hold the key to look all around yourself, and set its distance, offsets, aiming dot and use in seats.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Third Person gives you an over-the-shoulder camera. You turn it on and off with a key, a button or a console command. The camera stops at walls, so it never looks through them.

Hold the same key and move the mouse to turn the camera all the way around yourself (360 degrees), for example to see who is behind you. Your character keeps aiming where it was. Let go and the camera goes back.

With the aiming dot on, a small white dot shows where your shots actually go. The game's normal crosshair is hidden while you are in third person, because it points at the middle of the screen, not where you aim.

All settings in this panel are your own and are saved on your computer.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Third Person.

Everyone can use it while the server setting kgxat_thirdperson_all is 1 (the default). When it is 0, only toolbox admins can. The panel tells you which applies to you.

Other ways in:

  • Your toggle key. New players get H set once by the toolbox (change it in Settings > Keybinds).
  • The console command kgxat_thirdperson.
  • Third person on / off in the C menu.

If the server turned off third person in seats, the panel shows "(Third person in seats is turned off on this server.)".

How to use it

Set up a key and use it
  1. Open Utilities > Third Person.
  2. Click the key box under Key: tap = on / off, hold = look around and press the key you want, for example H.
  3. Close the menu and tap the key. The camera moves behind your shoulder (it switches when you let go of the key).
  4. Tap the key again to go back to first person.
Look all around you
  1. Hold your third person key (it works from first or third person).
  2. While holding it, move the mouse. The camera circles around you, up and down too. Your aim does not move.
  3. Let go of the key. The camera goes back to the view you had, and third person stays as it was.
Move the camera to the left shoulder
  1. Drag Shoulder offset (right) to a negative value, for example -20.
  2. Adjust Distance and Height offset to taste. The change shows at once.

Settings and commands

Setting or command Default What it does
kgxat_thirdperson_all 1 Server: 1 = every player can use third person, 0 = admins only. In Settings > Server Settings as "Every player can use third person (off = admins only)".
kgxat_thirdperson_seats 1 Server: 1 = third person also works in seats and chairs. Cars keep their own view.
kgxat_thirdperson Console: toggle third person.
kgxat_tp_key, kgxat_tp_distance, kgxat_tp_right, kgxat_tp_up, kgxat_tp_crosshair, kgxat_tp_seats, kgxat_tp_orbit see above Your own settings from this panel.

Tips and limits

  • Third person switches itself off while you are dead, spectating, ragdolled, viewing through another entity, or in a black box jail.
  • In vehicles it only works in seats and chairs (prisoner pods). Cars keep their own view.
  • If you are not allowed, turning it on shows "Third person is admin only on this server."
  • Looking around stops by itself if third person becomes not allowed for you (dead, spectating, ragdolled and the like). In cars it is not available, like third person.
  • While looking around, the aiming dot (when on) still shows where your shots go.
  • The key does nothing while you type in chat or a text box, or while the game menu or console is open.
