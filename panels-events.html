Events

Split players into colored teams, send them to an arena or lobby warp, and run last one standing rounds.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it

What it does

Events is a quick way to run a fight without setting up a game mode. You split players into 2 to 4 colored teams, move them to an arena, and start a last one standing round.

Teams get their team color as player color and see a "Team" box in the top left corner. Team mates' names show over their heads. When teams are cleared, players get their old color back.

A last one standing round freezes everyone at the arena for a countdown, then lets them fight. Whoever dies is out and respawns in the lobby. The last player alive wins, or the last team with players alive. Everyone sees the round state at the top of the screen.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Events.

Admins, or players ticked for Events (teams, arena, rounds) in Player Access. The arena and lobby are warp points made with the Warp Points tool (Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Warp Points).

The panel

Number of teams 2, 3 or 4 teams (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow). 2 teams
Include admins when splitting Off: admins are left out of the split and lose their team. On
Split all players into teams Shuffles every player into the teams evenly.
Clear teams Takes everyone out of the teams.

Player list Columns Player and Team. Right-click a player to pick a team or No team.
Arena and lobby
Option What it does Default
Arena warp The warp where rounds take place. (none)
Lobby warp Where players go after a round, and where out players respawn. (none)
Refresh warp list Reloads the warp names from the server.
Send players to the arena Moves players to the arena.
Send players to the lobby Moves players to the lobby.

With teams, team 1 starts at the warp <arena>_1, team 2 at <arena>_2, and so on, when those warps exist. Otherwise everyone starts at the arena warp. Without teams, the send buttons move everyone except you; with teams, everyone in a team.

Last one standing
Option What it does Default Range
Countdown (s) Players wait frozen this long before the fight. 10 0-60
Start health (0 = keep) Sets everyone's health and clears armor. 100 0-1000
Strip weapons for the round (given back after) Takes weapons at the start and returns them after. On
Weapon everyone gets (class, empty = none) A weapon class to hand out. weapon_crowbar
I take part too (only matters without teams) Includes you in a round without teams. Off
Start last one standing Starts the round.
End round Stops the round and returns players.

A status line under the buttons shows the round state and how many are left.

How to use it

Run a team fight
  1. Place two warps named for example arena_1 and arena_2, plus arena and lobby, with the Warp Points tool.
  2. Open Utilities > Events and click Refresh warp list.
  3. Set Number of teams and click Split all players into teams.
  4. Pick arena as Arena warp and lobby as Lobby warp.
  5. Set the countdown, health and weapon, then click Start last one standing.
  6. The last team with players alive wins. After 5 s everyone returns to the lobby.
Run a free-for-all round
  1. Click Clear teams so nobody is in a team.
  2. Pick the arena and lobby warps.
  3. Tick I take part too if you want to play.
  4. Click Start last one standing. The last player alive wins.

Tips and limits

  • A round needs at least 2 players, and with teams at least 2 teams with players.
  • Jailed players and players who are spectating are skipped.
  • Nobody takes damage during the countdown.
  • Each death is announced in chat with the number of players left.
  • If no arena warp is set, players are not moved; you get a hint.
  • If the weapon class does not exist, you get an error, but the round still starts.
  • Events rounds and Game Modes matches never run at the same time.
  • Team damage blocking does not apply to players in a Game Modes match; matches have their own teams.
  • Teams and warp choices are not saved; they last until the map changes.
