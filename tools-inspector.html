Inspector

Show details about players and entities.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

Inspector tells you what you are looking at. Aim at anything and the HUD shows its class, index and model, or a player's name, SteamID and rank.

Left-click opens a window with full details. Right-click and reload copy the most-used identifiers straight to your clipboard.

With Live on, the window keeps updating while you hold the tool. This is useful for watching speed, health or position change.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Properties > Inspector.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): What anyone could see (no SteamIDs, wallets or positions).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Open the info window for what you aim at
Right click Copy the SteamID (player) or model path (entity)
Reload Copy the SteamID64 (player) or class name (entity)

Options

Option What it does Default
Live (the window keeps updating while you hold the Inspector) Off: details are taken once and printed to the console. On: the window updates twice a second. Off

What the window shows

The window lists Property and Value columns. Double-click a row to copy its value.

Target Rows
Player Name, SteamID, SteamID64, Rank, Team, Health, Armor, Alive, Weapon, God mode, Noclip, Frozen, Cloaked, Walk / run / jump, Ping, Packet loss, Speed, On ground, In vehicle, Job, Wallet, Position
Entity Class, Model, Index, Owner, Health, Mass, Frozen, Material, Color, Collision group, Map entity, Speed, Parent, NPC state, Position, Angles

How to use it

Find a prop's model path
  1. Aim at the prop. The model path shows under the crosshair.
  2. Right-click to copy it.
Get a player's SteamID
  1. Aim at the player.
  2. Right-click for the SteamID, or reload for the SteamID64.
Watch a player or NPC live
  1. Tick Live.
  2. Left-click the target. A window opens at the right of the screen and updates twice a second.
  3. Hold C to click the window, for example to copy a row.
  4. To stop, left-click nothing, close the window or switch away from the Inspector.

Tips and limits

  • With Live off, every left-click also prints the details to your console.
  • The live window does not take the mouse until you hold C.
  • Bots show "bot" as their SteamID64.
  • The Inspector works on talking NPCs, vehicle pads, trashcans and logic nodes, which refuse most other tools.
