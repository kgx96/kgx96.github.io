Property

Group doors into houses, shops or offices that players buy or rent.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Property groups doors into a property: a house, shop or office. Players press Alt + E on one of its doors to buy or rent it. Owners use Alt + E to lock, unlock, give keys or sell.

A property has a name, a price, and optionally rent paid every period. You can block its doors while it is for sale, and limit who may buy it to certain jobs. Selling a bought property gives half the price back.

Map doors work as they are. Door Maker doors are made permanent by themselves when you add them (kgxat_property_autoperma). With that off, make them permanent first.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Property.

Toolbox admins can use it. Superadmins can also give it to players in Settings > Player Access. That tick also lets them evict, remove, rename and price every property, and reset all doors.

Admins only (v3.6.11.3): this tool sets up the server, so it cannot be given to players; it is greyed out in Settings > Player Access.

Controls

Key What it does
Left-click Add the aimed door to the selected property (or start a new one)
E + Left-click Block the aimed door until it is sold (a door of no property goes up for sale with the settings below); again to unblock
Right-click Take the aimed door out of its property
Reload Select the aimed door's property (to add more doors to it); aim at nothing to start a new one
E + Reload Apply the name, price, rent and lock settings to the aimed door's property

The screen shows Adding doors to: with the selected property's name, or Next door starts a new property.

Properties on this map

A list with the columns Name, Price and Owner. Rentals show the price per period, for example /24h. Unowned properties show for sale. Click Refresh to reload it.

Right-click a property for:

  • Go there, Rename..., Set price...
  • Evict the owner (when it has one)
  • Who may buy it (jobs)...
  • Lock its doors, Unlock its doors, Open all its doors, Close all its doors
  • Make it server property (never for sale) or Make it a normal property (for sale) (superadmins only)
  • Remove property (its doors become normal doors)

How to use it

Make a house for sale
  1. Enter the Name, Price and other settings.
  2. Aim at nothing and press reload. The screen shows Next door starts a new property.
  3. Left-click the front door. This creates the property and selects it.
  4. Left-click every other door of the house to add it.
  5. Check the list: the house shows for sale.
Make a rental apartment
  1. Tick Rent (the price is paid every period).
  2. Set Price to the rent and Rent period (hours, real time) to the period.
  3. Start a new property and click its doors as above.
  4. Rent is taken from the owner's wallet at the end of each period while they are online.
Block a single door until it is sold
  1. Hold E and left-click the door.
  2. A door of no property goes up for sale on its own with the panel settings and stays shut until bought.
  3. A door of a property that is for sale is blocked or unblocked by itself.
  4. Hold E and left-click again to unblock it.
Change an existing property
  1. Change the settings in the panel.
  2. Aim at one of its doors, hold E and press reload. The settings, jobs included, go to that property.
  3. To add doors, aim at one of its doors and press reload to select it, then left-click the new doors.

Settings and commands

Setting or command What it does Default Range
kgxat_property 1 = properties can be bought and rented in RP mode. Off with DarkRP. 1 0 to 1
kgxat_property_max Most properties one player may own or rent. 2 1 to 20
kgxat_property_admin_unlimited 1 = toolbox admins may own any number of properties. 0 0 to 1
kgxat_property_signs 1 = property doors show the property name as a sign, and the owner above it when owned. 0 0 to 1
kgxat_property_autoperma 1 = a Door Maker door added to a property is made permanent by itself. 1 0 to 1
kgxat_resetdoors health Every door's health back to full (owners kept).
kgxat_resetdoors states Every door closed with its normal lock (owners kept).

Tips and limits

  • Saved per map in properties_<map>.json.
  • Property is part of RP mode (Settings > Gameplay > Server mode). You can set properties up in any mode.
  • With DarkRP running, its own door system is used instead.
  • An owner who is offline at the end of a rent period, or cannot pay, loses the property.
  • Selling a rented or server property gives nothing back.
  • Server property is never for sale. Only superadmins lock, unlock or take it, and it cannot be lockpicked.
  • The jobs limit is ignored while jobs are off.
  • Owners and key holders lock and unlock with Keys (left and right click) and open the property window with reload.
  • Removing the last door from a property removes the property.
