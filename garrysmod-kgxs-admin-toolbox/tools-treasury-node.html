Treasury Node

Place the server treasury in the world: robbing it takes treasury money.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

A treasury node is the server treasury in the world (v3.6.21). It is a money source whose money is the treasury itself: a robbery takes a share of the treasury, at most a set amount, straight out of the treasury balance. Admins and jobs with the Treasury ability press E on it to open the treasury window.

A node is a visible model (v3.6.21.5: a white CRT monitor by default, with the treasury on its screen) or an invisible node. Invisible nodes are drawn only for admins holding this tool; players walk through them and rob them where they are (the sign above shows the spot, unless you turn the sign off).

Nodes are server-owned and kept for the map.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Economy > Treasury Node. Toolbox admins only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a treasury node (kept for the map). On a node: its robbery settings
Right click The robbery settings of the aimed node (Money Generator)
E + right click The screen settings of the aimed node (v3.6.21.5.1; in v3.6.21.5 this was right click). While you hold this tool, E on a node does nothing else (no treasury window, no HIDE / SHOW, no carrying).
Reload Remove the aimed node

While you hold the tool, the text under your crosshair shows the aimed node's line (name, money it counts from) and "(hidden)" for invisible nodes.

The screen (v3.6.21.5)

A new node shows the treasury on its screen. On the screen models the page sits on the model's own screen; on any other model it is drawn on the front of the model's box. Hold E and right-click a node with the tool for its screen settings (or Alt + E on the node > Screen settings, or Screen settings of this node... in its robbery window):

So by default players see the balance and admins and Treasury jobs the whole page: the latest changes name who was paid, so choose Everyone only if that is public on your server. Robbery settings of this node (Money Generator)... at the bottom opens its robbery window. E anywhere else on the node still opens the treasury window for admins and Treasury jobs. The page updates every few seconds while someone is near (each player asks for it; nobody far away gets anything). A hidden (invisible) node shows no screen. Nodes placed before v3.6.21.5 keep their model; those on a monitor model get a screen, the others none until you turn it on.

Robbery settings

Placing a node opens its settings window (the Money Generator window) with Money comes from set to The treasury itself:

Option Default
Most money it holds (the most a robbery counts from) 1,000,000
Share of the money a robbery takes (%) 10
How it is robbed Bypass, both (mechanical, then digital)
Seconds of holding the button 40
Seconds before it can be robbed again 1,800
Who may rob it Jobs with the Robber ability
Police and admins are told On

With these, a treasury of 5,000 gives a robber 500; a treasury of 50,000,000 gives at most 100,000 (10% of 1,000,000).

A node cannot be filled or emptied: it is the treasury. Each robbery is a Robbery line in the treasury log (!treasury) and in Utilities > Money Log.

How to use it

A city treasury robbers can hit
  1. Hold the tool, type City Treasury as the name and leave the model on Storage closet.
  2. Left-click the floor of the vault room. The settings window opens.
  3. Set Share of the money a robbery takes to 5 and Seconds before it can be robbed again to 3600, then Save.
An invisible node behind a map's vault door
  1. Tick Hidden and left-click inside the map's vault.
  2. Leave Sign above it on so robbers find the spot, or turn it off for a hidden target.

Alt + E (admins, v3.6.21.5.1)

Alt + E on a node opens a small window with Open the treasury, Money Generator (robbery settings) and Screen settings.

Tips and limits

  • Bank vaults are different: they hold their own share of every tax and fee (Store Maker > Bank vault; Settings > Taxes & Treasury > Bank vaults %). A treasury node holds nothing of its own.
  • A robbery never takes more than the treasury has.
  • Removing a node with Reload removes its money source too; the treasury keeps its money.
  • Screens are drawn up to 900 units away (kgxat_screen_distance, per player). Settings > Taxes & Treasury > Screens on treasury nodes and stores (kgxat_screens) turns every screen off.
