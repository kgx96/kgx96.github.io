Robbery

Server rules for robbing money sources: robbing on/off, police and admin alerts, the robbery marker, minimum police online and signs.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Money sources fill up with money and can be robbed. You make them with Economy > Money Generator. This page holds the server-wide rules for all of them, plus a list of every money source on the map.

Robbers hold left click on a money source with Hands, a Lockpick, a Keypad Hacker or the Mechanical & Digital Bypass (Weapons > ATB), whichever the source allows. When a robbery starts, the police and admins can be told, with a marker on screen where it is.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Robbery.

Only superadmins can change these settings. The money source list works for toolbox admins and for players given the Money Generator in Settings > Player Access.

Money sources on this map

The list shows Name, Money (in it / most), Robbed with and Kept as. Right-click a row for Fill it up, Empty it or Stop being a money source. Refresh the list reloads it.

How to use it

Set up police and robbers
  1. In Settings > Jobs, give the robber jobs the Robber ability and the police jobs the Police ability.
  2. Here, tick Jobs with the Police ability are told....
  3. Set Police that must be online... to, for example, 2, and click Apply the numbers above.
  4. Robberies now need two police online, and police see a marker when one starts.
Hide all money source signs
  1. Untick Signs above money sources....
  2. Stores, ATMs and banks still show the money source line in their window.
Test a robbery without a second player
  1. Fill a source: right-click it in the list > Fill it up.
  2. Use a Test Dummy: Utilities > Test Dummy, Shift + right-click the dummy, then "Rob what I aim at".

Tips and limits

  • Each money source has its own alert switch in its Money Generator settings. With it off, nobody is told, whatever this page says.
  • One source alerts at most once every 30 seconds. With the two-step Bypass, only the first step alerts.
  • Each source can also hide its own sign in its settings.
  • Nothing can be robbed while the economy is off.
  • Job skills ("Robbing money sources") make robbing faster and surer.
  • Money sources are saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/robbery_<map>.json.
  • How fast a source fills, how it is robbed and who may rob it are set per source with Economy > Money Generator.
