Eye Scanner

Eye scanners that open linked doors for the players, jobs and ranks you pick.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

The Eye Scanner (v3.6.21.1) works like a keypad without a code. A player presses E on it and looks at it while it scans their eye. If they may pass, it says ACCESS GRANTED and opens the doors linked to it; if not, ACCESS DENIED. It is a Combine button on the wall with a glowing lens, a scan beam and a sign on its front.

Who may pass is set on the scanner itself: picked players, jobs (a Mayor, the Police) and ranks. Its owner and admins always pass. A scanner can be locked down, alarm its owner and the police when someone is denied, and keep a list of the last scans.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Eye Scanner.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access; they link their own scanners to their own doors (a door they made, or of a property they own), within Settings > Limits > Eye scanners.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place an eye scanner on the aimed wall (it is selected)
Right click Select a scanner, keypad or button; then right-click doors to link or unlink them
Reload Open the aimed scanner's settings
Shift + reload Remove the aimed scanner (yours; admins: any, server scanners too)

On a scanner:

Key Who What it does
E Anyone Scan: stay within reach and look at it until it is done (the scan time).
Shift + E Its owner, admins Its settings window
Alt + E Admins The Access window: server owned, ranks / jobs, a name, and (v3.6.21.5.1) an Eye scanner settings button

The tool panel

Option What it does Default
Name shown on it Text on its sign, e.g. Police HQ. (none)
Who may pass (for new ones) The players, jobs and ranks picked in its settings; Anyone; or Only me (and admins). The picked ones
Seconds a scan takes 0.5 to 10. 2
Doors stay open Seconds the linked doors stay open; 0 = until the next pass (each pass opens, then closes them). 5
Attach to the prop it is placed on It moves with that prop. On
Server eye scanner (admins) Saved for the map, belongs to no player. Off

The settings window

Shift + E on the scanner (or reload with the tool). Three tabs:

Tab What it has
Who may pass Name shown on it, Who may pass (the players, jobs and ranks picked below / Anyone / Only me), Players (everyone online, and players picked before who are offline), Jobs, Ranks. Ctrl + click picks more than one.
Settings Seconds a scan takes, Doors stay open, Locked down (nobody passes but the owner and admins; it shows LOCKED DOWN), Tell me when someone is denied, Tell the police when someone is denied (jobs with the Police ability), Sounds, Can be broken into with (v3.6.21.2, below), Seconds to break in, Chance a break-in works, and how many doors are linked.
Scans The last 30 scans: when, who, Passed or Denied. Clear the list.

Save keeps the changes (server scanners stay saved for the map). The window is in each player's own language.

How to use it

A police station door only the police open
  1. Make the door with Building > Door Maker (or use a map door).
  2. Hold the Eye Scanner tool, type Police HQ as the name and left-click the wall next to the door.
  3. Right-click the door to link it.
  4. Shift + E on the scanner: on Who may pass, Ctrl + click Police in Jobs, then Save.
A private door for you and two friends
  1. Place a scanner and link your door.
  2. Shift + E: pick your friends in Players and Save.
  3. Tick Tell me when someone is denied to know who tries it.

Breaking in (v3.6.21.2)

Each scanner picks which tools break into it, in Shift + E > Settings > Can be broken into with:

Choice What works
Any (default) The Keypad Hacker, or the Bypass with either click
Keypad Hacker only Only the Keypad Hacker
Keypad Hacker or the digital Bypass The Keypad Hacker, or the Bypass held with right click
The Bypass only The Bypass, either click
The mechanical Bypass only The Bypass held with left click
The Bypass, both Left click (mechanical) first, then right click (digital) within the server's time (60 s). Each part takes half the break-in time. The hardest.
Nothing Nobody breaks in

Seconds to break in and Chance a break-in works are the scanner's own; 0 uses the server's (Settings > Lockpick & Hacking > Eye scanners: 25 seconds, 100%). Job skills make it faster, as with keypads. A finished break-in opens the linked doors, shows ACCESS GRANTED and is in the Scans list. The owner hears about it when it starts (kgxat_hack_alert).

Tips and limits

  • Moving away or looking away stops the scan ("Stay close and look at the scanner until it is done").
  • One scan at a time per scanner; the Access window's ranks and jobs (Alt + E) apply as well as the scanner's own list. A server scanner with no list of its own lets in whoever the Access window allows.
  • Breaking in: the Keypad Hacker and the Mechanical & Digital Bypass work on eye scanners the way Can be broken into with says (below), unless the scanner is locked down; admins' scanners only with kgxat_hack_admin.
  • Denied alarms reach the owner and the police at most every 10 seconds per player and scanner.
  • Eye scanners cannot be copied with the duplicator.
  • Server scanners keep their settings, open time and links over restarts.
