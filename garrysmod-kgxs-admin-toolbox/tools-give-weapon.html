Give Weapon

Give or take any weapon.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it
  3. Controls
  4. The panel
  5. How to use it

What it does

Give Weapon hands a weapon to a player. Pick it from the panel's searchable list or type its class. Reload takes that same weapon away.

If the player already has the weapon, giving it again gives them its ammo instead. The tool can also switch the player to the new weapon right away.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Cheats > Give Weapon.

Toolbox admins can use it. With a CAMI admin mod (ULX, SAM, sAdmin) that means the KGX Admin Toolbox privilege, default rank admin. Without one, admin and superadmin can use it. A superadmin can give it to non-admin players, or to one rank, in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Themselves, weapons the gamemode lets them spawn.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Give the selected weapon to the player you aim at
Right click Give the selected weapon to yourself
Reload Take the selected weapon from the player you aim at (or yourself)

While you hold the tool, the selected class shows below the crosshair as "Weapon: <class>".

The panel

Option What it does Default
Weapon class The weapon to give or take, by class name. weapon_crowbar
Switch to the weapon after giving The player switches to the weapon after they get it. On
Reset to defaults Puts both options back to their defaults.
Weapon list

Below the options are a search box (Search weapons...) and a list with the columns Weapon, Category and Class. It holds every installed weapon in the game's weapon list, sorted by category, then by name.

Typing in the search box filters by name, class and category. Clicking a row puts its class into Weapon class.

How to use it

Give a player a weapon
  1. Type part of the name in Search weapons..., for example "shotgun".
  2. Click the row you want. Its class appears in Weapon class.
  3. Aim at the player and left-click.
Give yourself a weapon by class
  1. Type the class into Weapon class, for example weapon_rpg.
  2. Right-click anywhere.
Take a weapon away
  1. Select the weapon in the list or type its class.
  2. Aim at the player and press reload. Aim at nothing to take it from yourself.
  3. If they do not have it, you get "... does not have <class>."

Tips and limits

  • The class must be a known scripted weapon, start with weapon_, or be in the weapon list. Otherwise you get "Unknown weapon class".
  • You cannot give a weapon to a dead player.
  • If the gamemode or another addon blocks the weapon, you get "Could not give ... (blocked by the gamemode or another addon)."
  • Works on players only. Rank immunity applies.
  • To give ammo for every carried weapon at once, use Cheats > Refill Ammo.
