Prop Protection

Built-in prop protection so players cannot move or tool each other's props, plus your buddy list and a cleanup button.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

With prop protection on, players cannot physgun, gravity gun, tool, drive or use context menu properties on other players' things. You can name buddies who may touch your props.

Toolbox admins are never blocked. This built-in protection stays off by itself while another prop protection addon is installed, for example FPP.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Prop Protection.

Every player can use the buddy list, the cleanup button and the owner hint. Only superadmins can change the two settings at the top.

The panel

Status

A colored box at the top shows the current state:

  • Green: "Prop protection is ON: players cannot move, tool or use other players' props (admins can)."
  • Red: "Prop protection is OFF: everyone can touch everything."
  • Amber: another prop protection addon is installed, so this one stays off.

How to use it

Turn prop protection on
  1. Log in as a superadmin and open Utilities > Prop Protection.
  2. Tick Prop protection on. The status box turns green.
  3. Tick Players may touch map props and props nobody owns if players should still move map props.
Build together with a friend
  1. Open Utilities > Prop Protection.
  2. Double-click your friend in the list. Buddy changes to Yes.
  3. Your friend can now move, tool and use your props until you leave.
Check who owns a prop
  1. Hold the physgun, gravity gun or tool gun.
  2. Aim at the prop. The owner shows next to your crosshair.
  3. Green means you can touch it. Red means you cannot. "Owner: nobody (map or world)" means it has no owner.

Settings and commands

Setting Default What it does
kgxat_pp 0 1 = built-in prop protection. Off while another prop protection is installed.
kgxat_pp_world 0 1 = with prop protection on, players may still move and tool map props and props without an owner.
kgxat_pp_hud 1 Client setting. Shows who owns what you aim at.

Tips and limits

  • Buddies only last until you leave the server. At most 64 buddies.
  • Being a buddy is one-way. Your buddy's props stay protected from you unless they add you too.
  • Ownership is set when a player spawns props, ragdolls, effects, entities, NPCs, vehicles and weapons, and for things made with tools such as wheels and thrusters.
  • Blocked players see "That belongs to (name)." or "You cannot touch map props here."
  • Toolbox admins ignore prop protection. Area tools skip props the player may not touch.
  • The owner hint only shows while prop protection is active.
