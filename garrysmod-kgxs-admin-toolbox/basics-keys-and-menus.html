Keys and Menus

Every key and menu the toolbox adds for players: backpack, third person, voice wheel, F3 hub, F4 jobs, Alt + E and more.

What it does

The toolbox adds keys and menus for players and admins. Some keys you can change in Settings > Keybinds; others are fixed, such as E, Alt + E, F3 and F4.

Most features behind these keys can be switched off by the server. A key does nothing when its feature is off, while you type, or while a menu is open.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Keybinds. Everyone can set their own keys there; the settings are saved only on your computer.

The server hub (F3) also has a keys tab. It shows the server's key list and the toolbox keys you have set.

How to use it

Set your third person key
  1. Open Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Keybinds.
  2. Click the box under Third person: tap on/off, hold to look around and press H.
  3. If a warning says the key is used by something else, pick another key.
  4. Tap H in game to switch third person on and off. Hold H and move the mouse to look all around you.
Put a tool on a key
  1. In the Keybinds panel, pick a tool in Quick tool 1.
  2. Click the box under Key for quick tool 1 (none by default) and press a key.
  3. Press that key in game; your tool gun switches to the tool.
Use the voice wheel
  1. Type !voice to pick the lines on your wheel.
  2. Hold K. The wheel opens in the middle of the screen.
  3. Point at a line and let go of K. Nearby players hear it.

Settings and commands

Setting or command What it does Default
kgxat_default_keys Server: 1 = players get the recommended toolbox keys once 1
kgxat_thirdperson_all Server: 1 = every player can use third person; 0 = toolbox admins only 1
kgxat_access_altuse Server: 1 = admins press Alt + E for the Access window 1
kgxat_cuffs_escape Server: 1 = handcuffed players may try to escape with a minigame (reload) 1
kgxat_backpack_key Client: backpack key B
kgxat_voice_key Client: voice wheel key K

These console commands can also be bound with bind <key> <command>: kgxat_thirdperson, kgxat_camera_toggle, kgxat_camera_next, kgxat_camera_remote, kgxat_vision_toggle, kgxat_spectate_stop, kgxat_backpack_open, kgxat_hub, kgxat_jobs, kgxat_loadout_menu, kgxat_patchnotes, kgxat_debug_console.

Tips and limits

  • Mouse buttons used as keys do nothing while the cursor is showing, for example in a menu.
  • The look-tool key is never left on G, Throw Equipped's key. If it was on G, it is cleared and you are told in chat.
  • Keypads can also be opened with E; typing the code on the number pad only works while you look at the keypad.
  • Third person also works in seats and chairs unless kgxat_thirdperson_seats or your own Third person: also while sitting is off. Cars keep their own view.
  • With the hub on, F4 does not open the jobs menu; use !jobs or the hub's jobs tab.
