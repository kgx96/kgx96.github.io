Jobs

Make the jobs players pick in RP mode: pay, model, weapons, slots, ranks, abilities, staff jobs, licenses and elected jobs.

What it does

Jobs is the editor for the toolbox's own RP jobs. A job sets a player's paycheck and what they are allowed to do. It is also a team, so it colors their name on the scoreboard and in chat.

A job can also give models, weapons, health and armor. Staff jobs are limited to admins and a whitelist, and elected jobs are won by a vote.

Players without a job are Unemployed, in their own grey team. Jobs only run when Settings > Gameplay > Server mode is RP, and they are off while DarkRP runs. You can still set them up in any mode.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Jobs.

Anyone can open the page, but only superadmins can change jobs and settings. Whitelists are only sent to superadmins.

Players pick a job in these places:

  • !jobs, /jobs or the console command kgxat_jobs. With the F3 hub on (the default), these open the hub's Jobs tab.
  • F4, when the F3 hub is turned off. It opens the Jobs window.
  • !job <name> takes a job by name. !job unemployed or !job none quits.
  • For players: the C menu's ATB window, Me tab > Jobs.
  • Talking NPCs can also give jobs, and admins can set anyone's job from the Player Manager (Set job).

The panel

Sliders in the first group only apply when you click Apply. Checkboxes and the Jobs list shows box apply at once. Every change to the job list is saved straight away.

How to use it

Make a new job
  1. Set Settings > Gameplay > Server mode to RP.
  2. Open Settings > Jobs and type a Name and Description.
  3. Fill in Model, Weapons, Paycheck (-1 = normal) and Slots (0 = no limit).
  4. Tick the abilities the job needs and pick a team color.
  5. Click Add as a new job. It is saved at once and shows in every player's job list.
Change or remove a job
  1. Click the job in the list. Its settings fill the form.
  2. Change what you need and click Update the selected job.
  3. To delete it, click Remove the selected job and confirm. Players in it become Unemployed.
Make a staff job
  1. Make or select a job and set Who can take it to Admin job (admins + whitelist).
  2. Open Add an online player to the whitelist and pick a player, or type SteamIDs.
  3. Leave Whitelisted players get toolbox admin access while in this job ticked to give them admin access in the job.
  4. Click Update the selected job.

Regular players see the job as Locked with the reason. A whitelisted player in the job uses the toolbox like an admin, but superadmin settings still need a real superadmin. Taking someone off the whitelist removes them from the job.

Make an elected job
  1. Select the job, tick Elected by vote (players run for it, everyone votes; Utilities > Voting settings) and click Update the selected job.
  2. When a player picks the job, chat says they are running. Others pick the job in the next kgxat_election_join seconds to run too (up to 6).
  3. Everyone then votes. With one candidate it is a yes / no vote. The winner gets the job if it is still free.

Tips and limits

  • At most 32 jobs. Jobs are saved in garrysmod/data/kgx_admin_toolbox/jobs.json.
  • A server with no jobs gets the preset jobs once. Add a preset job adds any of them again: Store Owner, Police Officer, Medic, Thief, Chef, Hobo, Hacker, Bank Robber and Black Market Dealer.
  • Taking a job (Settings > Gameplay > Job equip): by default the player dies dissolving and respawns where they stood (or at the job's spawn), so the new job's gear comes with a fresh spawn; the death takes nothing and does not count. Instant loadout freezes the player ("Changing job..."), respawns them in place and runs the gear check. Either way weapons bought in stores come back with their ammo. Not while jailed or handcuffed.
  • Players cannot change jobs while jailed. Admins skip the cooldown, rank limits and the license need.
  • Leaving RP mode or turning jobs off takes everyone out of their job.
  • With several looks, players pick one next to Become.
  • Spawn at uses World > Spawn Points names and Utilities > Warps. Maps without them use the normal spawns.
