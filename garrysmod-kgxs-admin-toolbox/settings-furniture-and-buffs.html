Furniture & Buffs

Server settings for trashcans, chairs players buy in stores, and buffs such as food, drink, poison and status effects.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

This page holds the server settings for three small systems. Trashcans let players throw away things from their backpack and pockets. Chairs can be bought by players in stores. Buffs are food, drink, poison and status effects made with World > Create Buffs.

Better Freeze and Create Chairs have no server settings, so they are not on this page.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Furniture & Buffs.

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings.

Related places:

  • Trashcans are placed with World > Make Trashcan. Players press E on one to open the Trash window.
  • Chairs are sold in stores as item type Chair (World > Store Maker, General Store preset).
  • Buffs are made with World > Create Buffs.

Checkboxes apply at once. Sliders apply when you click Apply the numbers above.

How to use it

Throw something away as a player
  1. Walk up to a trashcan and press E.
  2. In the Trash window, click Throw away next to an item under Your backpack or Your pockets.
  3. To get something back before it is deleted, click Take next to it under In the trash.
Limit chairs per player
  1. Move Chairs one player may own to the number you want.
  2. Click Apply the numbers above.
Make world food edible
  1. Make a buff in World > Create Buffs with the model of the prop.
  2. Make sure Buffs work and Alt + E on a prop with a buff's model eats / drinks it are ticked.
  3. Players press Alt + E on that prop to eat or drink it.

Settings and commands

Setting or command What it does Default Range
kgxat_trash_time Seconds until trash is deleted 120 0-3600
kgxat_trash_public Everyone sees and takes trash 1 0-1
kgxat_chairs_buy Chairs sold in stores 1 0-1
kgxat_chairs_max Chairs per player 4 1-20
kgxat_buffs Buffs work 1 0-1
kgxat_buffs_world Alt + E eats buff props 1 0-1
kgxat_buffs_map Map props can be eaten 1 0-1
kgxat_buffs_gibs Eaten props break into gibs 1 0-1

kgxat_buffs_stack Food items per stack 10 1-100
!removechairs Players: remove all your chairs
!mychairs Players: shows how many chairs you own
kgxat_buff <player> <buff id> Admins: give a player a buff

Tips and limits

  • Players' chairs are removed when they leave.
  • Map props that were eaten come back after a cleanup.
  • With Seconds until thrown away things are deleted at 0, things are deleted the moment they are thrown away.
  • kgxat_buff respects rank: you cannot target a player of a higher rank.
  • The Drugs settings (a buff kind) are on their own page, Settings > Drugs.
