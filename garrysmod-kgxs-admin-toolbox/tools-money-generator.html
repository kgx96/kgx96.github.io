Money Generator

Make the aimed thing a money source: it fills up with money and can be robbed.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

On this page

What it does

The Money Generator makes a money source out of whatever you aim at. A money source fills up with money every few seconds, up to its maximum. Robbers take the money out by holding the mouse button on it with the right tool.

Each source has its own settings: how fast it fills, how much it holds, how it is robbed, who may rob it, and which jobs may collect its money legally. You set them in a window that opens when you left-click.

Stores, ATMs and banks show the source as a line in their window. Everything else gets a sign above it with its name, the money inside and "Can be robbed".

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Economy > Money Generator.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it only if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access. Players use it only on their own props: their sources start empty, make at most 1,000 a minute and hold at most 50,000, cannot be filled, have no collecting jobs, and cannot be robbed or collected by the player who made them. They see, empty and remove only their own sources. Filling and emptying are written to the Action Log.

Admins can also click the money source line in a store, ATM or bank window to open its settings.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own props, limited, cannot be filled or robbed by them.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Make the aimed thing a money source, or change it (opens its settings)
Right click Fill the aimed money source up
Reload Empty the aimed money source

While you hold the tool, the text under your crosshair shows the aimed source's line, or "not a money source (left click)".

Buttons at the bottom: Make it (or Save for an existing source), Fill it up, Empty it and Stop. Stop asks first; the money in it is lost.

Bank vaults and treasury nodes (v3.6.21) work differently:

  • A bank vault's Fill it up takes the money from the treasury and Empty it gives it back; Stop, or making it a different kind of source, also gives its money back to the treasury. It cannot be collected.
  • A treasury node cannot be filled or emptied (it is the treasury: change the treasury in !treasury). Robbing it takes the share of the treasury (at most its most) and shows in the treasury log and the Money Log as Robbery.
  • Players' own sources always make their own money.
  • Bank vaults and the treasury only go on things kept for the map: a bank or ATM, a map entity, a permanent prop or a treasury node. Changing where a source's money comes from starts it empty.

The tool panel

The panel lists every money source on this map with the columns Name, Money, Robbed with and Kept as. Right-click a row for Fill it up, Empty it or Stop being a money source. Refresh the list reloads it.

How to use it

Make a robbable safe
  1. Spawn a safe prop and make it permanent with the Permanent Props tool, so it is kept for this map.
  2. Aim at it with the Money Generator and left-click.
  3. Type a Name, set the money per minute and the most it holds.
  4. Pick How it is robbed, for example Lockpick.
  5. Click Make it. The sign appears above it.
Let a job collect a store's takings
  1. Left-click the store, ATM or bank with the tool.
  2. Under Jobs that collect its money legally, pick the job that runs the store.
  3. Set Who may rob it to Nobody if it should never be robbed.
  4. Click Make it. Players with that job hold left click on it with Hands to collect.
Test a robbery
  1. Right-click the source to fill it up.
  2. Use a Test Dummy: Utilities > Test Dummy, Shift + right-click the dummy, then "Rob what I aim at".

Tips and limits

  • Kept for this map: map entities, permanent props, stores, ATMs, banks and talking NPCs. Any other entity stays a source only until it is removed, and is not saved.
  • Sources and the money in them are saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/robbery_<map>.json, every 2 minutes and at shutdown.
  • Right-click and Reload only work on something that is already a money source. They open the settings window afterwards.
  • Filling or emptying also clears the robbery cooldown.
  • Nothing can be robbed while the economy is off. Robbing can be switched off for the server in Settings > Robbery; collectors can still collect then.
  • Give robbers the Robber ability and police the Police ability in Settings > Jobs.
  • Server-wide alerts, the police marker, the minimum police count and signs are in Settings > Robbery.
  • The robbers' weapon is Weapons > ATB > Mechanical & Digital Bypass.
