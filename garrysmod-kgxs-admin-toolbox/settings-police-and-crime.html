Police & Crime

Server settings for the police batons, handcuffs, security cameras, pickpocketing, the Door Explosive, Poop and cigarettes.

What it does

This page holds the server settings for the toolbox's police and crime gear. That gear is in the spawn menu under Weapons > ATB: Police Jail Baton, Admin Jail Baton, Handcuffs, Door Explosive, Poop and Cigarettes. Pickpocketing is done with Hands.

Jobs get the matching abilities (Police, Pickpocket and Hobo) in Settings > Jobs. Police gear works for jobs with the Police ability and for toolbox admins.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Police & Crime.

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings.

Related places:

  • Police jails: Functions > Jail. Open a jail location's settings and tick "Police jail".
  • Cameras: Building > Cameras. The remote control key is set in Utilities > Cameras.
  • Cigarette Machine: spawn menu > Entities > ATB.

How to use it

Arrest a player as police
  1. Take the Handcuffs and left-click the player to cuff them. Right click uncuffs.
  2. Hold E on the cuffed player to drag them.
  3. Let go inside a jail. Choose Jail them here or Just let go (they stay handcuffed).
  4. Or take the Police Jail Baton, press reload to switch to Jail mode, and left-click the player.
Let a prisoner out with the baton (v3.6.21.5)
  1. Take the Police Jail Baton (or the Admin Jail Baton) and press reload until the mode reads Release.
  2. Left-click the jailed player, also through the cell bars. They are let out.
  3. With the Police Jail Baton this works on players the police jailed; others need an admin.
Set up police jails
  1. Open Functions > Jail and make a jail location.
  2. Open its settings and tick "Police jail".
  3. Jail hits now send players there for Seconds in jail seconds.
Pick a pocket as a player
  1. Take out Hands and crouch. The crouch HUD shows the eye: closed means nobody nearby looks at you.
  2. Get behind a player and aim at them: their name and your chance show under the crosshair.
  3. Right-click the player.
  4. The chance (default): stay crouched next to them until the bar fills; then the chance decides. Timing minigame: left-click while the marker is inside the green zone (right click stops); enough good hits take the money.
  5. Success takes part of their wallet. A failure warns the victim.
Break free of handcuffs
  1. Press reload while handcuffed.
  2. Left-click each time the marker is in the green zone, until you reach the needed hits.

Tips and limits

  • Admins can never be pickpocketed.
  • The job skill "Pickpocketing (Hands)" makes the green zone bigger and the marker slower.
  • Handcuffed players hold Hands only. They cannot use, pick up or switch anything, or use the tool gun or physgun.
  • The Admin Jail Baton is admin only and asks where to jail.
  • Release mode (v3.6.21.5): both batons can let jailed players out (a setting for each). They get their weapons back and, when set, go back where they were, as with any release.
  • The Door Explosive is sold as a black market shipment. Police and the door's owners defuse it with E.
  • Police buy Security Monitors in a store (Police Supply preset) and pick a police camera with E.
  • To test alone, use a Test Dummy: handcuff and drag it, Shift + right-click it for "Try to break free of the handcuffs", or give it money and pickpocket it.
