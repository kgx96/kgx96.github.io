Fireworks

Rockets that burst into colors, spark fountains and firework shows. They hurt nobody.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does

What it does

Fireworks (v3.6.21.3) launches rockets that rise with a spark trail and burst into colored light with a bang, puts fountains of sparks on the ground, and plays whole shows. Everyone on the server sees them. They are light only: they hurt nobody and break nothing.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Fun > Fireworks. Toolbox admins can use it; players if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Launch a firework from the aimed spot
Right click A fountain of sparks on the aimed spot
Reload A show: several fireworks around the aimed spot, one after another

The tool panel

Option What it does Default
Shape Random (a different one each), Sphere, Ring, Willow (gold, falling), Crackle, Two colors, Heart, Star. Hearts and stars face whoever watches. Random
Random colors Off: the color below. On
Color The firework color. Red
Height (units) How high it bursts. 1200
Size How big the burst is. 1
Show: fireworks How many a show launches (admins up to 40). 10
Show: spread around the spot (units) 300
Fountain: seconds How long a fountain sparks (admins up to 30). 8

How to use it

A New Year's show
  1. Pick Random shapes and Random colors.
  2. Set Show: fireworks to 30 and spread to 600.
  3. Aim at the middle of an open area and press reload.

Tips and limits

  • Players (Player Access): one firework a second, a fountain every 4 s, a show of up to 8 every 15 s, up to 2000 high and size 1.5.
  • Each firework is drawn on every player's own computer; many at once cost some frames on slow computers. At most 80 are drawn at once, and players more than 12000 units away do not draw them.
  • v3.6.21.4.1: fireworks are sent to every player and drawn by the toolbox itself (in v3.6.21.3 they used scripted effects that did not show). If one fails to draw, the console shows one "[KGX's Admin Toolbox] fireworks:" error line.
