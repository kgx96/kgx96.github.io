Door Maker

Turn any prop into a sliding, hinged or phasing door.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

Door Maker turns a prop into a door. A door can slide in a direction, rotate on a hinge, or phase: stay in place and turn see-through so people walk through it. The door stays frozen in place and moves only when opened or closed.

Directions are relative to where you look when you click. Left-click a door again with new settings to change it; it is measured from its closed position.

Props welded or constrained to a door move with it as one door. Pressing E on any piece, linking any piece to a button or keypad, or clicking any piece with the tool acts on the whole door.

A door belongs to whoever owns the prop. The owner locks and unlocks it with Keys.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Door Maker.

Admins can use it, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access. Players carrying Keys can also use it on their own props when kgxat_keys_doormaker is on. Their doors are theirs and count toward their door limit.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Doors from their own props only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Make the aimed prop a door (directions are relative to where you look)
E + Left click Weld: pick a base door/prop, then other props to weld onto it
Right click Open/close the aimed door (also map doors)
Reload Turn the aimed door back into a normal prop
E + Reload Clear the weld base

While you hold the tool, aiming at a door shows "Door (opens with E)" or "Door (button/keypad only)", its owner and whether it is locked.

How to use it

Make a sliding door
  1. Choose Slide and a Direction, for example Up.
  2. Aim at the prop and look the way it should move. Left-click.
  3. Right-click it to test, or press E on it.
Make a hinged door
  1. Choose Rotate (hinged), a Hinge and an Angle.
  2. Left-click the prop. Use a negative angle if it swings the wrong way.
Build a door from several props
  1. Hold E and left-click the main prop. It is the weld base (outlined white).
  2. Hold E and left-click each other piece. They are welded on.
  3. Left-click the base without E to make it a door. The welded pieces move with it.
  4. If the base is already a door, it rebuilds itself to include new pieces. Close it first.
Open a door from a distance
  1. Make the door.
  2. Use Building > Button or Building > Keypad and link it.

Keys and ownership

The owner, and players they give a key to, lock and unlock the door with Keys. Left click locks, right click unlocks, reload opens its key menu. A locked door opens with E only for them and admins. Buttons and keypads still open it.

Admin Keys and Functions > Keys work on every door and can change the owner. Owner, keys and lock stay when you change the door and are saved with permanent doors. Reload (back to a prop) clears them.

Admins: Alt + E on a door that is not in a property opens its Access window: server owned, which ranks or jobs may open it, and a name. Property doors open the property window instead.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_keys_doormaker 1 = players carrying Keys may use the Door Maker on their own props (Settings > Gameplay). 1 0-1
kgxat_limit_doors Most doors a limited player may have (Settings > Limits, "Doors (Door Maker)"). 10 0-100
kgxat_doors_frozen 1 = every door is frozen: nobody opens or closes doors with E, keys or Door Maker buttons; the Keys tool's buttons still work (Functions > Keys). 0 0-1

Tips and limits

  • Only props with physics can be doors. Map doors are already doors: link them with Button or Keypad.
  • Doors cannot be picked up with the physgun. Reload to turn it back into a prop first, then move it.
  • Precision Move, Rotate, Prop Reset, Align and Prop Scale work on doors. The door is closed first and still opens correctly afterwards.
  • To keep a door after a restart or cleanup, make it permanent (World > Permanent Entities). Its door settings are saved closed.
  • Undo removes a weld made with E + left click.
  • Players using it through Keys can only pick their own props: "With your Keys you can make doors from your own props only."
