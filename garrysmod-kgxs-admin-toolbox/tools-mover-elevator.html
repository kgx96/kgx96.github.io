Mover / Elevator

Make a prop move between two points: elevators, lifts, sliding platforms. Players standing on it ride along.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

Mover / Elevator turns a physics prop into a mover. It moves smoothly between where it is now (point A) and a second point (point B). Players and props standing on it ride along.

You set point B in one of two ways. Left-click uses a direction and distance from the panel. Right-click the prop, then right-click where its bottom should end up.

Players start a mover with E. Buttons and keypads link to a mover like to a door (Building > Button / Keypad).

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Mover / Elevator.

Admins can use it, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own props, up to the mover distance and speed caps.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Make the aimed prop a mover (direction and distance from the settings)
Right click Pick a prop, then right-click where its bottom should go (point B)
Reload Turn the aimed mover back into a normal prop (at point A)
E + Reload Start / trigger the aimed mover

While you hold the tool, a green beam shows where the aimed prop would go with the current settings. A picked prop is outlined yellow, with a yellow beam from its bottom to where you aim. Reload on anything that is not a mover clears the pick.

Options

Option What it does Default Range
Direction (left-click) Up, Down, Left, Right, Away from you or Toward you. Left, right, away and toward follow where you look. Up
Distance (left-click) How far it moves, in units. 128 4-16384
How it moves The mode (see below). Elevator: go, wait, come back
Speed (units per second) Travel speed. 100 5-1000
Wait at the ends (s) Pause at each end. 3 0-60
Players can press E on it Off = only buttons and keypads move it. On
Elevator sounds Start, move and stop sounds. On
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to its default.

The modes:

  • Elevator: go, wait, come back: one press sends it to B, it waits, then returns to A.
  • Toggle: each press goes to the other end: each press moves it to the other end.
  • Loop: back and forth on its own (press to pause): it starts at once, keeps moving between A and B and waits at each end. A press pauses it where it is; the next press starts it again.

How to use it

Make an elevator
  1. Set Direction (left-click) to Up and Distance (left-click) to the floor height, for example 256.
  2. Set How it moves to Elevator: go, wait, come back and Wait at the ends (s) to 3.
  3. Aim at a flat plate and left-click.
  4. Stand on it and press E. It goes up, waits, and comes back with you on it.
Move a platform to an exact spot
  1. Right-click the prop. It is outlined yellow: "Picked."
  2. Right-click the spot where its bottom should end up, for example a ledge on the upper floor.
  3. The prop is now a mover between its spot and that point.
Call it with a button
  1. Place a button with Building > Button. It is selected.
  2. Right-click the mover to link it.
  3. Press E on the button to start the mover.
Change or remove a mover
  1. Change the settings and left-click the mover again. It is made again from its start point.
  2. Press reload on it to turn it back into a normal prop at point A.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_limit_movers Most movers a limited player may have (Settings > Limits, "Movers / elevators"). 3 0-50

Tips and limits

  • Only physics props can be movers. Players, NPCs, vehicles and props attached to something else are refused.
  • A piece of a Door Maker door cannot be a mover. Remove the door first.
  • The distance must be at least 4 units.
  • To keep a mover after a restart or cleanup, make the prop permanent too (World > Permanent Entities), before or after. It is saved at point A.
  • Admins and superadmins are not limited by kgxat_limit_movers unless kgxat_limits_admins is on. Superadmins never are.
