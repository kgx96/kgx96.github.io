Game Modes

Run matches with teams, roles, rounds and win rules: 21 built-in modes from Team Deathmatch to Gun Game, Race, Prop Hunt and Survival (PVE waves), plus your own.

What it does

Game Modes is the toolbox's round engine. A mode says how a match is played: teams or free for all, roles with their own gear, a lobby, rounds, a win rule and respawn rules. One match runs at a time.

A match moves through phases: Lobby, Get ready, Live and Round over, repeated for each round. Players keep everything they had: weapons, health, armor, position, model and Build mode are given back when they leave or the match ends.

There are 21 built-in modes, and superadmins can edit them or build new ones. Spawns, lobby and spectator spots and objectives are placed per map with the World > Mode Spots tool.

On Sandbox and Build / PvP servers, everyone joins the lobby when it opens. On RP servers (kgxat_playmode 2) a match is an opt-in event round: only players who type !join take part. Players in and out of the match cannot hurt each other.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Game Modes.

  • Running matches: admins, or players ticked for Game Modes (start and run matches) in Player Access. Others see only the player commands.
  • Creating, editing, duplicating and deleting modes: superadmins only.
  • The server settings at the bottom: shown to everyone with access, changeable by superadmins only.
  • Players use chat commands (!join, !leave, !ready, !sitout, !class, !buy, !taunt). The C menu has Match: pick your class and Match: buy menu while you are in a match that uses them.

Prop Hunt prophunt Props hide as props; hunters wait 30 s blind, then hunt. Teams swap every round; 6 rounds.
Hide and Seek hideseek Seekers wait 40 s blind, then tag hiders by touch. Tagged hiders seek too. 5 rounds.
Freeze Tag freezetag Taggers freeze runners by touch; a free runner by a frozen one for 3 s thaws them. 5 rounds.
Last One Standing lms Free for all, one life. Last player alive wins the round; 3 rounds.
Capture the Flag ctf Two teams. Take the enemy flag to your own flag while yours is home; first to 3 captures. Needs a flag for each team.
King of the Hill koth Two teams. A team scores 1 every second it is alone on the hill; first to 120. Needs a hold zone.
Domination domination Two teams capture and hold points: 10 a capture, 1 per point every 2 s, 1 a kill; first to 250. Needs capture points.
Gun Game gungame Free for all. Every kill gives the next weapon of the ladder; a kill with the last one (the crowbar) wins.
Infection infection Survivors against the infected (20 % at the start). Dead survivors join the infected. 5 rounds, at least 3 players.
Juggernaut juggernaut Free for all with one Juggernaut (1000 health, pulse rifle, RPG). Only the Juggernaut's kills score; kill them to take over. First to 15.
Duels duels One against one, one life; the others watch. The winner stays, the loser goes to the back of the line. 10 rounds.
Race race Run the race checkpoints in order. The first to finish wins; the others get 30 s more. 3 rounds. Needs 2 or more checkpoints.
Survival survival v3.6.17, PVE. Everyone together against waves of zombies that grow and get tougher. Hits and kills give points for wall weapons, doors, the mystery box and perk machines (World > Buy Stations). The fallen come back when a wave is cleared; when everyone is down it is over. Loadouts: Both (server loadouts and custom classes). Needs enemy spawns.
Hidden-role modes (Traitors, Murder)
  • Roles are handed out when the round goes live and stay private. After each round everyone's role is shown.
  • Traitors: 25 % of players are traitors (at least one). From 6 players there is one detective per 8, known to all. Team chat reaches only traitors.
  • Traitors win when every innocent is dead; innocents when every traitor is dead or time runs out.
  • Press E on a body to search it: name, role, cause of death and the last person they looked at.
  • Traitors and detectives get 2 credits a round, plus 1 per kill of the other side, to spend with !buy.
  • Murder: the knife (weapon_crowbar) and the hidden gun (weapon_357) kill in one hit; nothing else hurts. A bystander who shoots a bystander drops the gun for the round.
  • During a round: no kill feed, no scores, and the dead cannot be heard or read by the living.
Hiding modes (Prop Hunt, Hide and Seek, Freeze Tag)
  • Team 1 hides, team 2 seeks. Seekers wait frozen and blind for the hide time; nobody is hurt meanwhile. Hiders win if one is still free at the end.
  • Prop Hunt: as a prop, aim at a prop (up to 96 units wide and high) and left-click to become it. Hits on your disguise hurt you. Hunters lose 5 health per wrong prop hit.
  • Hide and Seek and Freeze Tag: 25 % of players seek, picked each round. A seeker's hit is a tag, not damage. Tags and thaws do not work through walls.
  • !taunt makes a hider play a sound, once every 8 s. The match goes to the player who won the most rounds.
Classic modes (v3.6.10)
  • A mode whose The map needs setting asks for flags, a hold zone, capture points, bomb sites, an escort path or race checkpoints opens no lobby on a map without them. The message names what to place with World > Mode Spots, for that mode or for every mode.
  • Gun Game: everyone starts on the first weapon of the ladder and only holds that weapon. Each kill gives the next one; your score is the levels you gained. A kill with a crowbar, stunstick or fists takes a level from the victim (setting Gun Game: a melee kill takes a level from the victim). Dying by yourself costs nothing.
  • Infection: team 1 survives, team 2 infects. Players who join late are infected. No survivor left: the infected win. Time up with a survivor alive: the survivors win. The match goes to the player who won the most rounds.
  • Juggernaut: a random player becomes the Juggernaut when the round goes live, with the mode's health, 100 armor and the Juggernaut weapons. Everyone sees JUGGERNAUT over their head. Kills by others score nothing. Whoever kills the Juggernaut scores and takes over at once. A Juggernaut who falls or leaves is replaced about a second later.
  • Duels: the first two of the line fight; everyone else watches them. Newcomers join the end of the line. Time up: the duelist with more health wins (equal health is a draw).
  • Race: the checkpoints count in the order they were placed, for the mode's laps; the last checkpoint is the finish. Racers cannot hurt each other unless Race: racers may hurt each other is on. Die or fall and you come back at your last checkpoint. With Race: vehicle set, every racer gets that vehicle when the round goes live and after each respawn; only its owner can drive it.
  • The HUD shows each mode's state above the bottom of the screen: your level and next weapon, survivors left, the Juggernaut, the duel and who is next, and for a race the next checkpoint (a ring on the ground and its distance), lap and position.
Waves (PVE, v3.6.17)
  • Special rules = Waves (PVE) makes a mode where everyone plays together against NPCs: no teams, players cannot hurt each other, one long round with no time limit, no respawns during a wave.
  • Enemies come from the Enemy spawn spots (World > Mode Spots, for that mode or every mode); without one the lobby does not open. Wave 1 has enemies in the first wave, each wave more enemies each wave on top, at most most enemies alive at once at a time, and % more enemy health each wave more health. The NPC classes come in order: the first kind from wave 1, one more kind every 3 waves.
  • Points are the mode's match money: everyone starts with points everyone starts with, a hit gives points for a hit (once per enemy per tick, so a shotgun blast is one hit), a kill points for a kill. Players spend them at Buy Stations and in the buy menu (always open in Waves). They end with the match.
  • A player who goes down watches and comes back when the wave is cleared. Players who join during a wave wait (they cannot hurt the enemies or score) and play from the next wave, or at once when nobody else is left. When nobody is standing the match is over ("reached wave N"). With waves to survive above 0, surviving that many waves wins.
  • The enemies hate only the players in the match: hub players, spectators and the map's own NPCs are left alone (both ways), and they do not hurt each other. Map NPCs and wave enemies cannot hurt each other. They always hunt the nearest player in the match; the AI Brain and the NPC relationship rules leave them alone (no fleeing, surrendering or calling for help). One stuck far away for 30 s, or that neither hurts nor is hurt for 45 s while not moving (inside a wall, behind a closed door buy), is replaced at an enemy spawn. Headcrabs and torsos that come out of wave zombies count as wave enemies. They are removed when the match ends.
  • The enemy list takes NPC classes only: an entity that is no NPC (or no class at all) is skipped for the match with a console message. NPC kills count for points and the match scoreboard, not for Match stats kills, XP or credits.
  • NPCs that need a weapon (Combine soldiers, Metro Police, rebels) get weapon of enemies that need one.
  • The HUD shows the wave, the enemies left and the break countdown; the HUD & Hub token {x:wave} shows the wave number.
  • Match stats achievements: Hold the Line (standing when wave 10 starts) and Last One Out (wave 25).

The mode editor

Superadmins open it with New mode..., Edit... or Duplicate.... Click Save the mode to store it, or Cancel. The Id (letters, digits, _) is used by commands and Logic. It can only be set on a new mode. Saving a mode that is running does not change the running match.

Teams, roles and buy menu items
  • Teams (none = free for all): Add a team adds up to 8. Right-click a team for Rename..., Color, Team weapons (everyone in the team)... and Remove. One team becomes two.
  • Roles: Add a role adds up to 12. Each role has an id, a name, a team (or any), At most per team (0 = no limit), weapons, health, armor, speed (0.5-2), a player model and Spare magazines per weapon (default 4). Limited roles are handed out first at random; everyone else gets the first role with no limit.
  • Buy menu items: Add an item... adds up to 40. Kinds are Weapon, Ammo, Armor, Health, Radar and Disguise; each has a name, price, amount and Who may buy it (hidden-role modes). Use the default items (HL2 weapons, armor, health, ammo) and Use the Traitors equipment (prices in credits) fill the list.

How to use it

Run a quick match
  1. Open Utilities > Game Modes.
  2. Pick a mode in Mode, for example Team Deathmatch.
  3. Click Open a lobby for this mode. Everyone on the server joins the lobby.
  4. Players type !ready. The match starts once enough players are in, the lobby wait has passed and enough are ready.
  5. To skip the wait, click Start now. Click End the match to stop early.
Run an event round on an RP server
  1. Open a lobby as above. Everyone sees "Event: <mode>! Type !join to take part."
  2. Players who want to play type !join. Others get a chat reminder every 30 s while the lobby is open.
  3. Start the match when enough have joined. Players leave with !leave.
Let matches run by themselves
  1. In Utilities > Game Modes > Settings, set Next match to 1 (vote), 2 (rotation) or 3 (random), and press Apply the numbers.
  2. Untick In the next-mode vote and rotation in the editor of modes you do not want to come up.
  3. Place what modes need on each map with World > Mode Spots; modes a map cannot run are left out there.
  4. After each match ends, players see "Next mode vote in 20 seconds." and then vote with the number keys. The lobby of the winner opens.
  5. For a map vote now and then, set a map vote every N matches.
Run an arena with a hub and loadouts (v3.6.15)
  1. With World > Mode Spots, place a few Hub spots (where everyone waits between matches) and each mode's spawns.
  2. In Settings > Player Loadouts, make loadouts with Game mode set to a mode or Any match, and save.
  3. Edit the modes: Loadouts = Server loadouts (or Both to add the players' Armory classes, or Armory for classes only). Optional: paid loadouts, locked after the start, holding the loadout in the lobby.
  4. Set Next match so matches keep coming, and load [Built-in] PVP (arena) in Settings > HUD & Hub.
  5. Players spawn in the hub with their hands, get the loadout screen when a lobby opens, and come back to the hub after the match. During a match their backpack, pockets, containers and stores wait.
Run a Survival (PVE) server (v3.6.17)
  1. With World > Mode Spots, place Hub spots and, for Survival (or every mode), a few Enemy spawn spots away from the players' spawns.
  2. With World > Buy Stations, place wall weapons, a door buy, a perk machine and a mystery box (see its tutorial).
  3. In Utilities > Game Modes, take the other modes out of the rotation if you only want Survival, and set Next match so a new match follows each one.
  4. Load [Built-in] PVE (waves) in Settings > HUD & Hub: the card shows the points, the hub help explains the waves.
  5. Optional: set up Settings > Armory; Survival's Loadouts is Both (server loadouts and the players' own classes); set it to Armory or Server loadouts for one kind only.
Make your own mode
  1. As a superadmin, right-click a built-in mode and pick Duplicate..., or click New mode....
  2. Give it an Id and a Name, then set rounds, win rule and respawns.
  3. Add teams, roles and buy menu items as needed.
  4. Click Save the mode. It appears in the Mode list.
  5. Place spawns for it with World > Mode Spots on each map you use.

Tips and limits

  • v3.6.17: in the mode editor and its role and item windows, hover an option whose name is cut off to read it in full.
  • Modes are saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/game_modes.json, at most 64. Built-in modes added by an update appear once; a mode you deleted stays deleted.
  • Without Mode Spots, players spawn where the map puts them.
  • After kgxat_match_lobbymax seconds a lobby starts with enough players, ready or not, or is cancelled.
  • In the lobby players cannot fight each other. Nobody can switch to Build mode during a match.
  • A mode whose win rule cannot end a round gets a 600 s time limit.
  • Jailed players leave the match. Match deaths drop no backpack, money or pockets.
  • v3.6.15: during a match players cannot use their backpack, pockets, containers, vaults, stores, shipments or Talking NPC weapon / armor / health rewards. A mode without get-ready time gives 10 seconds of the live round to pick a loadout; paid loadouts bought in the lobby are taken from the starting match money. Weapons dropped by match players can only be picked up by match players and are removed when the match ends. Modes with their own weapons (Gun Game, Juggernaut, Race, Infection, hiding and hidden-role modes) never use loadouts.
  • Dead players spectate: attack and alt attack switch player, jump switches first person and chase.
  • Logic has a Match watcher node and a Game mode action, so you can react to rounds or rotate modes. Next match (v3.6.14) rotates or votes without logic.
  • The rotation remembers its place across map changes (data/kgx_admin_toolbox/match_cycle.json).
  • Matches give XP, levels, achievements and unlockable name colors, badges and trails, with leaderboards and a match history: see Match Stats and Progression.
