Spray Paint

Paint on walls and floors; everyone sees it.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Spray Paint puts the toolbox's spray paint on the tool gun. Hold left click to paint where you aim on walls, floors and other parts of the map. Everyone sees the paint, players who join later too.

Paint never goes on props, players or NPCs. Doors take paint only when the server allows it, and the paint then moves with the door.

It uses the same paint, limits and settings as the Spray Paint weapon (Weapons > ATB). The Sponge (Weapons > ATB) cleans paint away.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Properties > Spray Paint.

Admins only by default. A superadmin can give it to all players or to one rank in Settings > Player Access, or add it to a Tool License (Settings > Licenses). Players can also buy the Spray Paint weapon in stores with the Hardware Store preset.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Paint on the world (doors when allowed), up to their dot limit.

Controls

Key What it does
Left (hold) Paint where you aim.
Right Open the color and size picker.
Reload Remove your last stroke. Press again to remove the one before.

While the tool is out, the bottom of the screen shows your current color, the size, and a short key reminder.

The panel

Option What it does
Pick color and size Opens the same picker as right-click.

The picker window, Spray Paint, has a color mixer, eight preset colors, a Size slider (2 to 64) and a Use it button. Your choice is kept in kgxat_spray_color (default 224 48 48, red) and kgxat_spray_size (default 8).

How to use it

Paint a mark on a wall
  1. Take out the tool gun with Spray Paint selected.
  2. Right-click, pick a color and a size, and click Use it.
  3. Aim at a wall within reach and hold left click while you move the crosshair.
Fix a mistake
  1. Press reload. Your last stroke disappears and you see "Last stroke removed."
  2. Press reload again to remove earlier strokes, one per press.
Clear all paint on the map (admins)
  1. Open Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Paint, Notes & Mail.
  2. Click Remove all spray paint on the map. Or type kgxat_paint_clear in the console.

Settings and commands

These are in Settings > Paint, Notes & Mail, under "Spray paint". Only superadmins can change them.

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_spray Spray Paint works. 1 0-1
kgxat_spray_max Paint dots one player may have on the map. The oldest go first. 1500 50-20000
kgxat_spray_total Paint dots on the whole map. The oldest go first. 12000 500-50000
kgxat_spray_lifetime Seconds paint stays (0 = until cleaned or the map changes). 0 0-86400
kgxat_spray_reach How far away (units) you can spray. 150 40-600
kgxat_spray_interval Players: milliseconds between paint dots (lower = faster). 15 5-200
kgxat_spray_interval_admin Toolbox admins: milliseconds between paint dots. 8 5-200
kgxat_spray_fill Fast strokes are filled in, with no gaps between dots. 1 0-1
kgxat_spray_doors Spray paint sticks to doors too. 0 0-1
Command What it does
kgxat_paint_clear [SteamID] Admins: removes all paint, or one player's paint.
kgxat_fast_draw 0/1 On your own game: 1 (default) draws the paint of each area as one batch; 0 draws it dot by dot (as before v3.6.11.4) if it ever looks wrong.

Tips and limits

  • When you pass your dot limit, your oldest paint disappears as you spray. The map limit works the same way for everyone.
  • A map cleanup removes all paint.
  • Paint on a door is removed when the door is removed.
  • Reload only removes your own strokes.
