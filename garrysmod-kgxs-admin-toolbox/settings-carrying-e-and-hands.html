Carrying (E & Hands)

Let players hold E to carry props and shipments, and set how heavy things may be with and without Hands.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

With carrying on, a player holds E on a prop, a shipment or a shipment item to carry it in front of them. Letting go of E drops it. A short tap of E still uses things as before.

Weight decides what can be carried. Light things are carried with anything in your hands. Heavier ones need Hands out, and the heaviest cannot be carried at all.

If a pickup addon such as Pickup Equipped is installed, it can take over carrying instead. This page decides who handles it.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Carrying (E & Hands).

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings.

Hands is a weapon in the spawn menu under Weapons > ATB. Every player carries it while kgxat_hands is on.

The panel

Checkboxes and the drop-down apply at once. Sliders apply when you click Apply the numbers above.

Option What it does Default
Carrying with E is on Master switch. On
Who carries things with E Automatic: a pickup addon (Pickup Equipped and similar) takes over when installed, Always the toolbox, or Never the toolbox (the game's own E pickup). Automatic

Apply the numbers above Sends the two sliders.

How to use it

Carry a prop as a player
  1. Look at a prop within reach.
  2. Hold E for a moment. The prop floats in front of you and follows your aim.
  3. Let go of E to drop it.
  4. If you see "Too heavy without Hands", switch to Hands and try again.
Handle shipments
  1. Tap E on a shipment to buy or take one.
  2. Press Alt + E for the shipment window.
  3. Hold E to carry the box. On a dedicated box or store, holding E carries one of its items.
Let a pickup addon do the carrying
  1. Leave Who carries things with E on Automatic. The toolbox steps aside when it finds the addon.
  2. If your addon is not found, choose Never the toolbox (the game's own E pickup).

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_carry Carrying with E is on 1 0-1
kgxat_carry_mode 0 automatic, 1 always the toolbox, 2 never the toolbox 0 0-2
kgxat_carry_weight Heaviest (kg) without Hands 35 1-1000
kgxat_carry_hands_weight Heaviest (kg) with Hands out 150 1-5000
kgxat_carry_admins Admins carry anything 1 0-1

Tips and limits

  • Never carried: players, NPCs, vehicles, doors, frozen things, welded or attached things, and the toolbox's stores, containers, talking NPCs, keypads, buttons, cameras, teleporters and vehicle pads.
  • A carried thing passes through the carrier, so it does not push them around.
  • Someone grabbing it with the physgun takes it from the carrier.
  • The carried thing is dropped when the carrier dies, enters a vehicle, or it gets too far away.
  • While the toolbox carries, the game's own E pickup of light props is replaced.
  • Pickup addons are found by name ("pickupequip" in their global table or hook names).
