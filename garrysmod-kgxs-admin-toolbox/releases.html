New: the toolbox in Q menu > Utilities > KGX

Logic for every part of the toolbox (World > Logic)

Fix

  • A spawn-menu hook returned a value, which in Garry's Mod stops the other map-start hooks: depending on their order, the toolbox's translation edits, owner list, weather and economy timers could be skipped (since v3.5.36).

Without KGX Core 1.3.3 the Display and Translations pages work as before. No tool changed.
