Cameras

Watch placed security cameras in a live viewport on your screen, switch between them, and turn them with the remote control.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

The Cameras panel shows a live picture from a placed camera in a small viewport on your screen. The viewport has the camera's name, a blinking REC mark and the time. You can show and hide it with keys and step through every camera you may view.

The remote control lets you turn the shown camera with the arrow keys, 360 degrees left and right and up and down. The camera itself does not move, only where it looks. Camera TVs that show the same camera turn with it.

Cameras are placed with the Cameras tool (Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Cameras). This page covers the viewing panel, not the tool.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Cameras.

Everyone can open the panel. Which cameras you see depends on each camera's access:

  • Private: its owner and admins.
  • Public: everyone.
  • Police: police and admins.
  • Admins only: admins.
  • A camera with ranks or jobs set in its Access window follows that list.

Who may turn a camera with the remote: its owner and admins. For police cameras, every officer too. The server setting kgxat_cam_remote must be 1.

You can also use your keys (set here or in Settings > Keybinds) and the console commands below.

The panel

Option What it does Default Range
Camera list Columns #, Name and Owner. Double-click a camera to show it.
Show selected camera Shows the selected camera in the viewport.
Hide viewport Hides the viewport.
Refresh list Reloads the list of cameras you may view.
Show/hide viewport key (recommended: N) Key that shows or hides the viewport. Clear key removes it. none
Next camera key (recommended: M) Key that shows the next camera you may view. none
Remote control on/off key: arrow keys turn the shown camera (recommended: J) Key that turns the remote control on and off. none
Remote control on / off Turns the remote control on or off.
Turn the shown camera straight again Points the shown camera straight ahead again.
Viewport width (px) Width of the viewport. Height follows at 16:9. 400 160-1000
Viewport position Top right, Top left, Bottom right or Bottom left. Top right
Reset to defaults Clears the three keys and resets the size and position.

How to use it

Watch a camera
  1. Open Utilities > Cameras.
  2. Double-click a camera in the list, or select it and click Show selected camera.
  3. The picture appears in the corner of your screen.
  4. Click Hide viewport to close it.
Flip through cameras with keys
  1. Bind Show/hide viewport key to N and Next camera key to M.
  2. Press N to show the viewport, and M to go to the next camera.
Turn a camera
  1. Show a camera you may control.
  2. Bind the remote key to J, or click Remote control on / off.
  3. The viewport shows "REMOTE: arrow keys turn". Use the arrow keys to look around.
  4. While the remote is on, the arrow keys do nothing else.
  5. Click Turn the shown camera straight again to reset the view.

Settings and commands

Setting or command Default What it does
kgxat_cam_remote 1 Server: 1 = cameras can be turned with the remote control by who may control them.
kgxat_camera_toggle Console: show or hide the viewport.
kgxat_camera_next Console: show the next camera.
kgxat_camera_remote Console: remote control on or off.
kgxat_cam_key, kgxat_cam_nextkey, kgxat_cam_remotekey, kgxat_cam_size, kgxat_cam_corner see above Your own settings from this panel.

Tips and limits

  • With no camera to show you get "No camera to show. Place one with Building > Cameras."
  • If you may view but not turn a camera, the viewport shows "REMOTE: you cannot turn this camera".
  • Up and down turning stops at 80 degrees.
  • The viewport hides itself when the camera is removed.
  • Arrow keys do not turn the camera while you type in a text box or the game menu is open.
  • The server's camera settings (camera TVs, police Security Monitors, the remote control) are in Settings > Police & Crime.
