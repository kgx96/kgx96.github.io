Lockpick & Hacking

Set how long the Lockpick and Keypad Hacker take, how often they work, which doors and keypads they open, and who may use them.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

The Lockpick opens locked doors and the Keypad Hacker opens keypads. Both are weapons in the spawn menu under Weapons > ATB. The player holds left click on a locked door or a keypad until the bar is full.

A picked door is unlocked and opened. A hacked keypad opens its doors as if the code was entered. This page sets the times, the success chance, which doors count, and whether only certain jobs may use them.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Lockpick & Hacking.

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings.

The jobs that may use the tools get the Lockpicking and Keypad hacking abilities in Settings > Jobs. The preset jobs Thief (Lockpick) and Hacker (Keypad Hacker) come with them.

How to use it

Pick a lock as a player
  1. Take the Lockpick from Weapons > ATB.
  2. Aim at a locked door and hold left click.
  3. Keep holding and aiming until the bar reaches 100%.
  4. On success you see "Lock picked." and the door opens. On a miss, "The pick slipped. Try again."
Hack a keypad as a player
  1. Take the Keypad Hacker.
  2. Aim at a keypad and hold left click until the bar is full.
  3. On success the keypad's doors open as if the code was entered.
Limit the tools to criminal jobs
  1. Tick Only jobs with the Lockpicking ability (and admins) can use it and Only jobs with the Keypad hacking ability (and admins) can use it.
  2. In Settings > Jobs, tick Lockpicking or Keypad hacking on the jobs that should have them.
Make lockpicking harder
  1. Raise Seconds to pick a lock and lower Chance that a finished pick works (%).
  2. Click Apply the numbers above.

Tips and limits

  • Job skills change both tools: a skill of 2 is twice as fast with twice the chance (at most 100%). Set them per job in Settings > Jobs.
  • A picked door locks itself again after the relock time, unless the owner changed the lock.
  • Letting go of left click or looking away stops the bar.
  • Map doors only count when they were locked with Admin Keys.
