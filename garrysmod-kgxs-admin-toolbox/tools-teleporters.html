Teleporters

Pads or zones that teleport players who walk into them.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does

What it does

Teleporters sends players somewhere when they walk into it. A teleporter is either a pad (a plate on the ground) or a zone (no prop, only a glowing ring).

A teleporter goes to one of two destinations. A linked pair connects an entrance to an exit, one-way or two-way. A warp teleporter sends players to a saved warp point.

Blue teleporters are active. Orange ones are exit only and send nobody anywhere. Permanent teleporters are saved for the map and come back after map cleanups and restarts.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Teleporters.

Admins can use it, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access. Players can only go to, rename or remove their own teleporters (from the list and with right click), and send teleporters only to public warps.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Placed where they stand, small pads and zones, public warps only, never saved for the map; their own teleporters only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a teleporter (pairs: entrance first, then exit)
Right click Remove the teleporter nearest to where you aim
Reload Cancel an unfinished pair

While the tool is out, a yellow ring at your crosshair shows the trigger radius. The text under the crosshair says "Place the ENTRANCE", "Now place the EXIT (reload cancels)" or the warp new teleporters go to.

Teleporters on this map

The list shows Name, Goes to and Saved for every teleporter. Goes to reads "warp: name", "pair", or "exit only".

Control What it does
Double-click a row Go there.
Right-click a row Go there, Rename... or Remove.
Rename selected teleporter... Names the selected teleporter. Empty clears the name.
Refresh list Reloads the list.

How to use it

Link two rooms both ways
  1. Set Destination to Linked pair and tick Two-way pair.
  2. Left-click the floor in the first room. You get "Entrance placed. Left-click again to place the exit."
  3. Left-click the floor in the second room. Both teleporters turn blue.
Send players to a warp point
  1. Make the warp first with Functions > Warp Points.
  2. Set Destination to Saved warp point and click the warp in the list.
  3. Left-click where the teleporter should go.
Cancel or remove
  1. Press reload to remove an entrance whose exit you have not placed.
  2. Right-click near any teleporter to remove the nearest one within 96 units.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_limit_teleporters Most teleporters a non-admin player may have (Settings > Limits). 0 = not allowed. 6 0 to 100

Tips and limits

  • Permanent teleporters are stored in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/teleporters_<map>.json.
  • A player who arrives on a teleporter must step out of it before it can send them again.
  • Dead, jailed, ragdolled and spectating players, and players in vehicles, are not teleported.
  • A teleporter frozen with Functions > Freeze does not send anyone.
  • Nobody can pick up teleporters with the physgun or gravity gun.
  • Removing one teleporter of a pair turns its partner into an exit only (orange).
  • Left click with Saved warp point and no warp picked gives "Pick a warp in the tool menu first."
  • A pad lies flat on the aimed surface.
  • Teleporters that are not permanent can be removed with your undo key.
