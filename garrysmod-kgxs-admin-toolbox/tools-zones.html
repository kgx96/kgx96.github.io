Zones

Safe, heal, no-build, spawn, hurt, instakill, forcefield (factions, props, bullets) and sound/music areas: preset shapes or drawn with nodes, saved for this map.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Zones marks an area of the map and gives it a rule. A zone can be a preset circle, square or rectangle, or any shape you draw with nodes. Zones are saved for this map.

Players inside a zone see its name at the top of the screen. All zones are shown while this tool is out. Show border to everyone also shows a zone's border to players.

Reload on an existing zone updates it with the current panel settings, so you can change a zone's type or rules without redrawing it.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Zones.

Toolbox admins can use it. Superadmins can also give it to players in Settings > Player Access, with a limit on how many zones each player owns. Players find, change and remove only the zones they placed.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Sound and no-build zones, small, near them.

Controls

Key What it does
Left-click Preset: place the zone where you aim. Nodes: add a node (click the first node to finish)
Right-click Nodes: finish the shape (3+ nodes). Otherwise: remove the zone you aim at
Reload Nodes: remove the last node. Otherwise: update the aimed zone with the current settings
E + Reload Cancel all nodes

The zone types

Type What it does
Safe Nothing inside takes damage: players, NPCs, props and vehicles. Players and NPCs inside cannot hurt anything outside.
Heal Heals players inside by Heal / hurt per second HP every second, up to their max health.
No-Build Non-admins cannot spawn things while standing in it or aiming into it.
Spawn zone Players spawn inside it. Nobody inside can be hurt or hurt anyone. Props can be spawned and moved.
Hurt Deals Heal / hurt per second damage every second. A safe zone wins over it.
Forcefield Only the factions you tick can enter. Can also keep props out and stop bullets.
Sound / music Loops a sound or web stream for players inside. Fades out when they leave.
Instakill Dissolves anyone who enters, like a pulse rifle energy ball, even in god mode.
Buff Gives the chosen buff every Heal / hurt per second seconds (food, poison gas, drunk...).

Zone list

Reset to defaults restores every option. Below it, a list shows all zones on this map with #, Zone and Distance. Double-click to go there. Right-click for Go there, Rename..., Copy position and Remove. Refresh and Remove all zones on this map... sit under the list.

How to use it

Make a safe zone around spawn
  1. Set Type to Safe and Shape to Preset: Circle.
  2. Set Circle radius. The outline at your crosshair shows exactly where the zone will go.
  3. Left-click the center of the area.
Draw a zone around an odd-shaped room
  1. Set Shape to Draw with nodes (any shape).
  2. Left-click points around the area, up to 32.
  3. Click the first node again, or right-click, to finish.
  4. Reload removes the last node. E + reload clears them all.
Keep monsters in a pen
  1. Set Type to Forcefield and Preset to Hostile NPCs only (monster pen).
  2. Untick Let bullets through the wall if players should not shoot in.
  3. Place the zone around the pen.
Change an existing zone
  1. Change the panel settings.
  2. Aim into the zone and press reload. It keeps its shape and takes the new settings.
  3. Circle zones also take the current Circle radius.

Tips and limits

  • Saved per map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/zones_<map>.json. A map holds at most 50 zones.
  • A drawn zone's floor is its lowest node and it reaches up by Height. Each node keeps its own height, so zones follow slopes and stairs.
  • A drawn shape that is too small or flat is refused. Spread the nodes out.
  • Admins can always build in no-build zones and are not limited in spawn zones.
  • Admins in noclip always pass forcefields and are safe in instakill zones.
  • A safe zone wins over a hurt or instakill zone.
  • In forcefields, NPCs count as friendly, neutral or hostile by how they feel about players; NextBots count as hostile. A driver brings their vehicle in or keeps it out.
  • Explosions, rockets and grenades are not bullets; forcefields do not stop them.
  • Forcefields made before v3.5.2 let props in until you reload on them with Let in: Props unticked.
  • Admin Vision (Utilities > Admin Vision) can show every zone.
