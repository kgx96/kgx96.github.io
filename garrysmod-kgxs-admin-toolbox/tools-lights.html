Lights

Glowing shapes in any size and color that light the world. Move them with the physgun.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Lights (v3.6.21.1) are glowing shapes that light up the world around them. Pick a shape and size, a color (and a second one), how bright it is and how far it lights. Give it an effect (pulse, flicker, strobe, color cycle, fade, candle) and a motion (bob, circle, figure 8, wander, spin). Move it with the physgun. Its body can be hidden, so only its light shows, until you hold the tool gun.

The server sets safety limits in Settings > Lights: the biggest size, the brightest and the farthest reach, how fast strobes and flickers may flash (or no flashing at all), and how many lights light the world at once.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Lights.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it if a superadmin ticks it in Settings > Player Access, up to Settings > Limits > Lights (10 each by default).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a light; on a light: give it the tool's settings
Right click Copy the aimed light's settings into the tool
Reload Remove the aimed light (yours; admins: any)
E on a light Switch it on or off (its owner and admins; anyone if allowed)

How to use it

A floating lamp over a table
  1. Shape Ball, size 0.5, a warm color, brightness 2, How far it lights 200.
  2. Left-click above the table. Grab it with the physgun to put it where you want; it stays there when you let go.
A police light bar
  1. Shape Flat panel, Color red, Second color blue, Effect Fade between the two colors, Effect speed 6.
  2. Place two on the roof of a car or building.
Hidden mood lights
  1. Shape Point (no body), tick Hidden body, a soft color, Effect Candle.
  2. Place them in corners. Players see only the light; you see the points while holding the tool gun.

Tips and limits

  • The game can show about 32 world-lighting lights at once; the nearest ones light the world (Settings > Lights sets how many), the rest still glow.
  • Each player can turn flashing off for themselves, or stop lights lighting the world (Settings > Lights > Your own view).
  • Players walk through lights unless Settings > Lights says they collide; they cannot push others with them.
  • The caps hold for lights already placed; raising a cap again gives them their own settings back.
  • Lights cannot be copied with the duplicator (a server light would be saved twice).

Fixed in v3.6.21.3

  • A light whose size was changed could not be picked with the tool (reload to remove, left click to change, right click to copy). The tool now also finds a light when you aim close to its middle.
