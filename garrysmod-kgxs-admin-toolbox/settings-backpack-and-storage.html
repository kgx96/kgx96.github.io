Backpack & Storage

Set up every player's backpack, the hotbar, Hands and storage containers: slots, what fits, and what happens on death.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

The backpack is every player's inventory. It holds weapons (with their clip ammo), ammo and items such as health kits. Loot from storage containers goes into it, and money goes to the wallet.

This page also controls the hotbar and Hands. The hotbar is ten slots at the bottom of the screen that replace the weapon selection. Only weapons on it can be used, and players set it up in the backpack window.

The last section turns storage containers on or off and sets how they look. Containers themselves are made in World > Storage and World > Loot Tables.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Backpack & Storage.

Anyone can open the page. Only superadmins can change the settings.

Players open their backpack with the B key, !backpack, !bp, the console command kgxat_backpack_open, or (for players) the C menu's ATB window, Me tab > Backpack. The key can be changed in Settings > Keybinds.

The panel

Checkboxes and the drop-down apply at once. The three sliders apply when you click Apply the numbers above at the bottom of the page. The blacklist has its own Apply the list button.

How to use it

Give players bigger backpacks
  1. Open Settings > Backpack & Storage as a superadmin.
  2. Move Slots to the size you want.
  3. Scroll down and click Apply the numbers above.
Make backpacks lootable on death
  1. Set When a player dies to Drop it as a bag anyone can loot.
  2. Set Dropped bag stays (minutes) and click Apply the numbers above.
Stop a weapon being stored
  1. Type the class in Never storable (weapon/item classes, comma separated), for example weapon_rpg.
  2. Click Apply the list.
Check or clean a player's backpack
  1. Open the Player Manager and choose Backpack > View backpack on the player.
  2. Select an item and click Remove selected item.
  3. To clear it all, choose Backpack > Empty backpack... and confirm.

Tips and limits

  • v3.6.15: during a Game Modes match the backpack is closed and nothing goes into it (the hotbar keeps an 11th weapon in hand), so match weapons cannot leave the match.
  • Admins keep their backpack on death unless Settings > Economy > Admins also drop money and their backpack when they die is ticked.
  • Deaths during a Game Modes match take nothing from the backpack.
  • Backpacks are saved per SteamID in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/backpacks.json.
  • Typed slider values are not sent until you click Apply the numbers above, which sits below the storage checkboxes.
