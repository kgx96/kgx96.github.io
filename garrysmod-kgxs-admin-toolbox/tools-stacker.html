Stacker

Stack frozen copies of a prop in any direction.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

On this page

What it does

Stacker makes copies of a prop and lines them up next to it. One click makes up to 20 copies in a row, in the direction you choose.

Directions follow the prop's own sides, so a tilted prop stacks along its tilt. Each copy is placed one prop-length further, plus the gap you set.

Copies keep the model, skin, color and material. They are always frozen, and one undo removes the whole stack.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Building > Stacker.

Admins can use it, and players who are given it in Settings > Player Access. Players can only stack props they are allowed to touch under the toolbox's prop protection.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Copies of their own props (game prop limit).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Stack copies of the aimed prop

Options

Option What it does Default
Copies How many copies one click makes. Slider 1 to 20. 1
Direction Up, Down, Front, Back, Right or Left, on the prop's own sides. Up
Gap between copies Extra space between copies. Negative values overlap them. Slider -512 to 512. 0
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to its default.

How to use it

Build a wall from one block
  1. Set Copies to the number of blocks you need.
  2. Set Direction to Right or Left.
  3. Left-click the block. The copies line up along its side.
Space out fence posts
  1. Set Direction to the side the row should grow.
  2. Set Gap between copies, for example 64.
  3. Left-click the first post.
Remove a stack
  1. Press your undo key once. The whole stack ("KGX Stack") is removed.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_limit_stack Most copies per click for limited players (Settings > Limits). 0 = not allowed. 5 0 to 20
kgxat_limits_admins 1 = limits also apply to toolbox admins. Superadmins are never limited. 0 0 to 1

Tips and limits

  • Works on regular props (prop_physics kinds) and prop_dynamic. The copies are always prop_physics.
  • The original prop is not moved or frozen. Only the copies are.
  • Copies belong to you and are cleaned up with your props.
  • A limited player who asks for more copies than kgxat_limit_stack is refused.
