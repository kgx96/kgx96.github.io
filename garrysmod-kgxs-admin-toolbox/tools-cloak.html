Cloak

Make players invisible and ignored by NPCs.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Cloak makes a player invisible. It hides their model, their shadow and the weapons they carry, and NPCs ignore them. Each click toggles it on or off.

Non-admins do not see a cloaked player's name when they aim at them. Toolbox admins still do. Footsteps, voice and chat are not hidden.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Cheats > Cloak.

Toolbox admins can use it. With a CAMI admin mod (ULX, SAM, sAdmin) that means the KGX Admin Toolbox privilege, default rank admin. Without one, admin and superadmin can use it. A superadmin can give it to non-admin players, or to one rank, in Settings > Player Access.

Other ways to toggle cloak:

  • Utilities > Player Manager: right-click a player > Toggle cloak. The player list shows "cloaked" in their status.
  • C menu: the ATB window, Me tab > Cloak on / off (yourself only).
  • Chat: !cloak <name>. Use ^ for yourself and @ for the player you aim at.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Themselves only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Toggle cloak on the player you aim at
Right click Toggle cloak on yourself

The panel

The panel has no options. It lists what cloak hides and what it does not.

How to use it

Watch the server unseen
  1. Select Cloak in the Q menu.
  2. Right-click anywhere. You are cloaked.
  3. Right-click again to show yourself.
Hide another player
  1. Aim at the player and left-click.
  2. Left-click them again, or let them respawn, to uncloak them.

Settings and commands

Setting / command What it does Default
!cloak <name> Toggle cloak from chat. Needs kgxat_chat_commands on and access to the Player Manager.
kgxat_chat_commands Admin chat commands such as !cloak work. 1

Tips and limits

  • Cloak is removed when the player respawns.
  • Weapons picked up while cloaked are hidden too. A dropped weapon becomes visible again.
  • A Player Effects trail is hidden while the player is cloaked.
  • Look-at details above players are not shown to non-admins for a cloaked player.
  • Standing up from Ragdoll keeps cloak.
  • Works on players only. Rank immunity applies.
