Jail

Lock players in a cell where they stand, or take them to a jail location.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

Jail locks a player in a cell. You can jail them where they stand, or take them to a jail location you set up on the map, like a sit room. A jailed player who leaves the cell is pulled back.

Jailed players cannot noclip, spawn things, use tools, use the physgun or enter vehicles. Their weapons are taken and come back on release. By default they are frozen and can only look around.

Jail survives death, reconnecting, map changes and restarts (saved in jail_pending.json; after a map change the player is jailed again by this map's jail rules; not after 30 days away). kgxat_unjailid <SteamID> (server console or a superadmin) releases someone who is offline. A timed jail shows a countdown to the prisoner and to admins, and the time continues where it stopped after a rejoin.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Jail.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. Rank immunity applies. The Jail locations (superadmins) settings in the panel can only be changed by superadmins.

Other ways in:

  • Utilities > Player Manager: right-click a player > Toggle jail or Jail at.
  • Chat: !jail <name> toggles jail (needs kgxat_chat_commands on).

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Where the target stands, 10 s to 5 min, only with kgxat_grant_others; release only their own.

How to use it

Jail a player where they stand
  1. Hold E and left-click the player.
  2. Bars appear around them. Right-click them to release.
Set up jail locations
  1. Stand in the middle of the cell or room and face the way prisoners should look.
  2. Press Add a jail location where I stand... and give it a name.
  3. Pick the type and other settings in the window that opens, then Save.
  4. As a superadmin, tick Take jailed players to a jail location (when this map has one).
Jail at a chosen place
  1. Press Reload until the HUD shows the choice you want.
  2. With Ask (menu), left-click the player. A menu lists the jail locations, Where they stand, Automatic and Cancel.
  3. With any other choice, left-click jails them there at once.
Draw a jail of any shape
  1. Set Tool does to Draw a jail with nodes (any shape) and type a name.
  2. Left-click points around the area, up to 32.
  3. Click the first node again or right-click to finish.
  4. Set Tool does back to Jail players.

Tips and limits

  • A cell holds one prisoner. When every cell is taken, the player is jailed where they stand with a notice.
  • A jail room or drawn jail holds any number of players, each on their own spot.
  • Jail locations are saved per map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/jails_<map>.json.
  • Locations show while you hold the tool and with Admin Vision markers.
  • You cannot jail yourself with the tool. Use Player Manager > right-click yourself > Toggle jail, then !unjailme.
  • Jumpscares and the black box can be switched for someone already jailed: Player Manager > right-click a jailed player.
  • With superadmin override on (kgxat_superadmin_override), E + Reload releases everyone on the first press.
