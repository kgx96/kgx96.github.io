Moderation

Slay, kick, strip, respawn, ignite, extinguish, slap, blind or break.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Moderation puts nine actions on one tool. You pick an action, then left-click the target. Right-click switches to the next action.

Most actions work on players. Slay also works on NPCs, and Ignite and Extinguish also work on NPCs and props. Break works on breakable things: props with health, glass, crates and func_breakable. Break never affects players or NPCs.

Reload breaks everything breakable in a radius.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Moderation.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. Rank immunity applies: you cannot act on a player ranked above you.

The same player actions are in Utilities > Player Manager (right-click a player) and in chat commands.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Slap, ignite, extinguish (others with kgxat_grant_others), break their own things; never slay or respawn.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Use the selected action on what you aim at
Right click Switch to the next action
Reload Break everything breakable in the radius

The HUD shows "Action: <name>" under the crosshair.

The actions

Action What it does
Slay Kills the player or NPC
Kick Kicks the player with the kick reason. Needs a second left-click on the same player within 3 seconds.
Strip weapons Takes all the player's weapons
Respawn Respawns the player
Ignite (10 s) Sets the target on fire for 10 seconds
Extinguish Puts out the fire
Slap Pushes the player in a random direction and does 5 damage
Blind (toggle) Turns a black screen on or off for the player
Break (breakable props, glass, crates) Breaks the one breakable thing you aim at

Options

Option What it does Default
Action The action left-click uses Slay
Kick reason Reason shown to kicked players Kicked by an admin
Break radius (reload) Area for Reload, 16 to 8192 units 256
Area break includes glass Area break also breaks map glass On
Respect Unbreakable (skip props made Unbreakable) Skips props marked Unbreakable On
Reset to defaults Puts every option back -

How to use it

Kick a player
  1. Pick Kick in Action, or right-click until the HUD shows it.
  2. Type a Kick reason.
  3. Left-click the player. A notice asks you to click again.
  4. Left-click the same player within 3 seconds.
Blind a player
  1. Select Blind (toggle).
  2. Left-click the player. Their screen goes black.
  3. Left-click them again to end it.
Clear glass and crates in an area
  1. Set Break radius (reload) and the two checkboxes.
  2. Aim at the middle of the area and press Reload.
  3. The action log records how many objects broke.

Settings and commands

Setting or command What it does Default / range
kgxat_cap_area_radius Largest area radius for limited players (Settings > Limits) 512, 0 to 16384
!slay, !kick, !strip, !respawn, !slap, !blind <name> The same actions from chat (needs kgxat_chat_commands on) -
kgxat_unblock <name> Server console or superadmin: clears blind and other blocks -
kgxat_unblockme Any toolbox admin, own console: clears blind and other blocks on yourself -

Tips and limits

  • You cannot kick or blind yourself.
  • Slay on a dead player gives "<name> is already dead." Slap does nothing on a dead player.
  • Area break only takes what prop protection lets you touch.
  • With Respect Unbreakable on, a prop marked Unbreakable gives "<name> is marked Unbreakable."
  • With superadmin override on (kgxat_superadmin_override), Kick works on the first click.
  • Every action is written to the action log.
