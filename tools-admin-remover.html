Admin Remover

Remove anything the right way: props, NPCs, vehicles, map props, permanent things and every toolbox thing.

What it does

Admin Remover removes anything except players and items a player is holding. That covers props, ragdolls, NPCs, vehicles, map props and every toolbox thing. Toolbox things go together with their saved data: stores, containers, talking NPCs, logic nodes and walls, signs, chairs, teleporters, notes, Door Maker doors, movers, server keypads, buttons, cameras and sound emitters.

Right-click also removes everything constrained or attached to the target, such as keypads on a door. Reload clears an area by filter, and Shift + Reload removes everything one player spawned.

E + left-click puts back your last removal of plain props, ragdolls, NPCs and vehicles.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Admin Remover.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. Players given the tool remove only what prop protection lets them touch, never toolbox, permanent or map things, and no areas. Their Shift + reload removes only what they spawned themselves.

Other way in: C menu, right-click an entity > ATB > Remove or Remove for good (toolbox things with their saved data, permanent and map things).

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own things only.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Remove what you aim at (or the sound emitter you aim at)
E + Left click Put back your last removal (plain props, ragdolls, NPCs, vehicles)
Right click Remove what you aim at, everything constrained to it and everything attached to it (keypads, buttons...)
Reload Remove everything in the radius that matches the filter (reload twice)
Shift + Reload Remove everything spawned by the aimed player or the aimed entity's owner (twice)

The aimed thing is outlined red. The HUD shows "Remove: <name>" with its kind, such as store, permanent, Door Maker door, server owned or map.

Area filters for Remove:

  • Props only: props, not map props.
  • Ragdolls
  • NPCs
  • Vehicles
  • Entities (not props / NPCs / vehicles)
  • Anything a player spawned
  • Everything (not players or map parts)

How to use it

Remove a contraption
  1. Aim at any part of it.
  2. Right-click. A notice reports how many things were removed.
Clear all ragdolls in an area
  1. Set Radius and set Remove to Ragdolls.
  2. Aim at the middle of the area and press Reload. A notice asks you to confirm.
  3. Press Reload again within 3 seconds.
Remove everything one player spawned
  1. Aim at the player or at something they own.
  2. Hold Shift and press Reload. A notice asks you to confirm.
  3. Press Shift + Reload again within 3 seconds.
Undo a mistake
  1. Hold E and left-click.
  2. Your last removal of plain props, ragdolls, NPCs or vehicles comes back.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default / range
kgxat_superadmin_override Superadmins skip the toolbox safety checks, including the second press for area and owner removal and rank immunity 0, 0 or 1

Tips and limits

  • Area removal never takes things created by the map, whatever the filter. Remove those one at a time.
  • Put back keeps your last 10 removals for this session. Toolbox things are not put back.
  • Server owned things have no player owner. Shift + Reload on one tells you to use left or right click instead.
  • E + Reload also removes everything the owner spawned, like Shift + Reload.
  • Rank immunity applies to the owner in Shift + Reload.
  • Bots are refused; use the Test Dummy tool. Held weapons are refused; use Moderation > Strip weapons.
  • Left-clicking the world removes the sound emitter you aim at (admins).
