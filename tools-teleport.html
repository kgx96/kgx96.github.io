Teleport

Move players and entities anywhere.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it
  3. Controls
  4. How to use it
  5. Settings and commands

What it does

Teleport moves players and entities. You mark a target, then click where it should go. You can also jump to any spot you aim at, or pull a target to you.

Players are placed where they fit, so they do not end up stuck in walls. A player in a vehicle is pulled out first.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Functions > Teleport.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. Rank immunity applies: you cannot mark or move a player ranked above you.

Other ways in:

  • C menu: right-click an entity > ATB > Go to it or Bring it in front of me.
  • Chat: !goto <name>, !bring <name> and !return [name] (needs kgxat_chat_commands on).
  • Utilities > Player Manager: right-click a player for Go to and Bring.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Within 256 units (others with kgxat_grant_others); never held (jailed, arrested, cuffed) players.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Aim at a player or entity to mark it. Aim at a spot to send the marked one there.
Right click Teleport yourself to where you aim
Reload Bring the aimed (or marked) player or entity in front of you

How to use it

Send a player somewhere
  1. Left-click the player. They are outlined in blue and a notice says they are marked.
  2. Left-click the floor or wall where they should go.
  3. They appear at that spot, placed where they fit.
Bring someone to you
  1. Aim at the player or entity and press Reload.
  2. They appear about 90 units in front of you.
  3. If you already marked a target, Reload without aiming at anything brings the marked one.
Teleport yourself
  1. Aim at any surface.
  2. Right-click. You move to that spot.

Settings and commands

Command What it does
!goto <name> Go to a player
!bring <name> Bring a player to you
!return [name] Send a player back to where they were before !goto or !bring

Chat command targets: part of a name, ^ for yourself, @ for the player you aim at. Use quotes for names with spaces.

Tips and limits

  • Switching to another tool clears the mark.
  • Clicking the sky does nothing.
  • If you left-click a spot without a mark, you get "Left-click a player or entity first to mark it."
  • Reload does nothing on yourself.
  • !return only knows positions saved by !goto, !bring or the Player Manager. Moves made with this tool are not saved for !return.
  • Every move is written to the action log.
