45 PANELS

Every panel under Q menu > KGX’s Admin Toolbox > Settings. Most are for admins, a few for superadmins only; each tutorial says who can use it.

Furniture & BuffsServer settings for trashcans, chairs players buy in stores, and buffs such as food, drink, poison and status effects.Tutorial GameplayPick the server mode (Sandbox, Build / PvP or
