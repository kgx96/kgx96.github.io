7 DAYS TO DIE MOD

KGX Wildlife.How to use it.

KGX Wildlife gives deer herds, hunting packs and animal populations per biome. These guides cover finding herds, reading the populations, predators and the tools for server owners.

Tutorial 01

Install and check KGX Wildlife

Get the herds, hunts and populations running.

  1. Install KGX Core (7DTD) 1.2.0 or newer, then extract KGX Wildlife into Mods so you have Mods\KGX_Wildlife.
  2. Load a new or existing world, press F1 and type kgx mods: kgx.wildlife should be listed as running.
  3. Head into the pine forest, burnt forest or snow, away from traders and towns. Deer now spawn there by day and by night, in herds of about three.
  4. Type kgx wildlife here to see the populations of the biome you are in. A species shows up once an animal of it has been seen there.

Tip: Animals spawn some distance away from you, in areas you have not just been through. Keep moving through new ground to find them.

Tutorial 02

Hunt deer herds

How herds move and react.

  1. Each herd follows its lead animal, a stag when the herd has one. A deer that falls behind walks back to it, or runs when it is far behind.
  2. Scare one deer and the whole herd runs: a shot, a player, a zombie, a wolf, a boar or a snake.
  3. Get close slowly and from behind cover. The first shot sends the rest of the herd away, so pick your target before you fire.

Tutorial 03

Read the populations and avoid overhunting

Every biome remembers how hard it has been hunted.

  1. kgx wildlife here shows each species in your biome: Thriving, Healthy, Thinning, Scarce or Very scarce, with its level (1.00 = normal).
  2. Every animal you kill lowers its species in that biome (by 0.04 with the default settings). A toast warns you when your kill makes a species thin out, get scarce or very scarce.
  3. A scarce species spawns less, its herds come smaller, and the animals left see you from further away and run further.
  4. Leave it alone and it recovers: every game day it gets part of the way back to normal. Or hunt in another biome for a while.
  5. kgx wildlife status lists the populations of every biome.

Tip: Above normal (Thriving), herds are bigger and other animals sometimes spawn with a companion.

Tutorial 04

Predators and the food chain

Wolves and mountain lions hunt too.

  1. Hungry wolves and mountain lions go after deer, rabbits and chickens nearby; dire wolves go after deer; coyotes after rabbits and chickens.
  2. Wolves nearby join the same hunt as a pack. A predator that has eaten rests for a couple of in-game hours.
  3. Animals that predators kill lower the prey's population a little and feed the predators. Where predators are many, prey settle lower; where prey are scarce, predators become fewer.
  4. Predators still go for you first when they see you, as in the vanilla game.

Tip: In the vanilla game, wolves, mountain lions and bears spawn in the snow, and wolves and mountain lions also in the desert at night. The pine forest has none of them.

Tutorial 05

Server owner: settings and admin commands

Tune the wildlife for your server.

  1. Settings (F2 or kgx config wildlife): herds, herdSize, hunting, huntRange, huntChance, populations, huntImpact, predationImpact, recoveryPerDay, dailyChange, minPopulation, maxPopulation, wariness, waryBoost and scarcityMessages.
  2. kgx wildlife set pine_forest deer 0.5 sets a population (1.0 = normal); all works for the biome or the species.
  3. kgx wildlife reset puts every population back to normal; kgx wildlife reset snow only one biome.
  4. kgx wildlife stats shows the animals, herds and hunts right now; kgx debug kgx.wildlife on writes every spawn, hunt and population change to the game log.
  5. Populations are saved per world in the world's KGX\kgx.wildlife folder. Admin commands need permission level 0; change it with kgx perms set wildlife.admin <level>.
