NPC AI

See every NPC on the map, switch NPC AI on or off, edit, heal, freeze or remove NPCs, and set profiles for NPC tags.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

NPC AI lists every NPC on the map, nearest first, with its health, order, tag and distance. Right-click an NPC to go to it, bring it, edit it, heal it, freeze its AI, release its orders, kill it or remove it.

The switches at the top control NPCs server-wide: turn all AI off, make NPCs ignore players, stop orders from the AI tools, and set the NPC difficulty.

The NPC tags section gives each tag a profile: a job, an attitude to players, running away or giving up, and a weapon. Every NPC with the tag gets the profile, also after it respawns.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > AI > NPC AI.

Admins can use it. Other players can use it if they are given NPC AI (NPC list, AI switches, NPC editor) in Settings > Player Access.

Right-click an NPC for Go to, Bring, Edit..., Heal, Freeze / unfreeze AI, Release orders, Kill and Remove. Double-click an NPC to go to it.

Button What it does
Refresh Reloads the list and the switches

Heal every NPC Heals every NPC to its max health

Edit... opens the same window as the NPC Editor tool: health, max health, weapon, accuracy, squad, size, AI frozen, and the AI Brain fields (name, tag, job, seconds between tasks, runs away, gives up, self-destruct).

How to use it

Freeze every NPC for a moment
  1. Tick AI off (every NPC stands still: ai_disabled).
  2. Untick it to let them move again.
Clean up spawned NPCs
  1. Click Remove every NPC (not the map's).
  2. Confirm with Remove.
Find and fix one NPC
  1. Find it in the list; the nearest NPC is on top.
  2. Right-click it and pick Edit....
  3. Change what you need and click Save.
Make a tag of friendly police
  1. Give NPCs a tag, for example guards, with the NPC Editor, an NPC Spawner or a Talking NPC with a real NPC body.
  2. In NPC tags, type guards in Tag, set Job (AI Brain) to Police and Attitude to players to Friendly.
  3. Click Save the tag's profile. The tagged NPCs show "Job: Police" on their card.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default
kgxat_ai_orders 1 = NPCs carry out orders from the AI tools 1
ai_disabled Game setting: 1 = NPC AI off 0
ai_ignoreplayers Game setting: 1 = NPCs ignore players 0
skill Game setting: NPC difficulty 1-3

Tips and limits

  • The list shows at most 300 NPCs.
  • Tag profiles are saved per map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/aitags_<map>.json, at most 64 profiles.
  • Tags use letters, digits, _ and -, up to 32 characters, in lower case.
  • The profile's weapon is given only to NPCs that have no weapon.
  • Apply again... with an empty Tag field applies to every tagged NPC.
  • Orders given by hand (Commander, AI Paths) are kept when a profile is saved. Only Apply again... replaces them.
  • AI off, ignore players, difficulty, and the three "every NPC" buttons are written to the Action Log, as are saved and deleted tag profiles.
