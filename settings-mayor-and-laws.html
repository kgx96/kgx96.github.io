Mayor & Laws

The Mayor's commands: laws and the law board, lockdowns, wanted players and search warrants; the server's own laws.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

On this page

  1. What it does
  2. Where to find it
  3. The panel
  4. How to use it

What it does

Settings > Mayor & Laws (v3.6.21.4) sets up the Mayor. A player in a job with the Mayor ability (Settings > Jobs; there is a ready-made Mayor preset job) and toolbox admins use chat commands that start with "!":

Command Who What it does
!addlaw <law> Mayor, admins Adds a law to the laws (and every law board); everyone is told
!removelaw <number> Mayor, admins Removes that law
!resetlaws Mayor, admins Back to the server's laws
!laws Everyone A window with the laws, the lockdown, who is wanted and search warrants (also E on a law board)
!placelaws Mayor, admins Puts a law board where you aim. A mayor has one (placing again moves it); admins' boards are the server's, saved for the map
!lockdown [why] Mayor, admins A lockdown: a siren, and a red banner tells everyone to get inside
!unlockdown Mayor, admins Ends the lockdown
!wanted <name>, <why> Mayor, admins, police (a setting) The player is wanted: everyone is told, WANTED shows over their head
!unwanted <name>[, why] Same No longer wanted
!warrant <name>, <why> Mayor, admins A search warrant: the police may open that player's property doors
!unwarrant <name> Mayor, admins Lifts the warrant

Names can be part of a name; use a comma between the name and the reason (!wanted carl, robbed the bank), or quotes for names with spaces or commas (!wanted "John Doe" robbed the bank). Without either, the first word is the name. One command a second per player.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Mayor & Laws. Only superadmins change the server settings; admins edit the laws now.

The server's laws: one per line. A new mayor starts with them, and !resetlaws goes back to them. Use the example laws fills in six example laws (then Save the server's laws):

  1. Call the Mayor "Boss". If you do not, it is AOS/KOS.
  2. Guns out is KOS/AOS. This does not apply to law enforcement.
  3. Walking in the street for longer than 10 seconds is KOS/AOS after a warning.
  4. You need a hit to kill the Mayor.
  5. People may be checked for any reason at any time.
  6. If you have a problem with a law, talk to the Boss.

The laws now (admins): right-click a law to remove it, type a law and press Enter to add it, Back to the server's laws, Clear the laws, Show the laws window.

How to use it

Set up a Mayor
  1. Settings > Jobs > Add a preset job > Mayor (Mayor and Treasury abilities, one at a time).
  2. Here: Use the example laws, then Save the server's laws.
  3. The Mayor types !placelaws at the town hall wall.

Tips and limits

  • A wanted player who is jailed (arrested) is no longer wanted. Leaving and coming back does not clear it.
  • Nobody clears their own wanted status or warrant (a wanted officer or mayor needs someone else).
  • Laws, lockdown reasons and wanted reasons go through the chat filter. Gagged players cannot use the commands.
  • After a restart or map change with no mayor, the laws go back to the server's laws a minute after the map starts.
  • The ATB window has Laws, and for the Mayor: lockdown on / off and the law board (they can be HUD shortcuts too).
  • Laws are kept over restarts (data/kgx_admin_toolbox/laws.json).
