SYS//BREAK

SYS//BREAK is a hacking game played through KGX//OS, a terminal and desktop inspired by the ship computers of classic sci-fi, guided by its system AI, ORACLE. Every network, address and file in it is made up. In development with Godot; not on any platform yet.
