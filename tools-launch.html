Launch

Fling players, NPCs and props.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

Launch throws the player, NPC or prop you aim at with the force you set. You choose the direction: straight up, away from you, a random direction, or straight down.

Right-click launches you instead. Frozen props are unfrozen before they fly.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Fun > Launch.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Themselves (no higher than the jump cap) and their own props (others with kgxat_grant_others); never held players.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Launch the player/entity you aim at
Right click Launch yourself

Options

Option What it does Default Range
Direction Straight up, Away from you, Random direction or Slam down Straight up -
Force Launch speed 1000 50-10000
Reset to defaults Puts both options back to the defaults - -

How each direction works:

  • Straight up: straight into the air.
  • Away from you: away from where you stand and upward. When you launch yourself, it goes the way you look, slightly upward.
  • Random direction: a random direction that always has some upward lift.
  • Slam down: straight down.

How to use it

Send a player flying
  1. Set Direction to Straight up and Force to 1000 or more.
  2. Aim at the player and left-click.
Knock a prop away from you
  1. Set Direction to Away from you.
  2. Aim at the prop and left-click. A frozen prop is unfrozen and flies.
Launch yourself
  1. Pick a direction and force.
  2. Right-click. You do not need to aim at anything.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_cap_launch_force Highest force a limited player's Launch gets (Settings > Limits, superadmins only) 1500 0-10000
kgxat_cap_jump_power Highest force a limited player launches themselves with (v3.6.11.3) 300 0-10000

Limited players are players given the tool, and admins when kgxat_limits_admins is 1. Superadmins are never limited.

Tips and limits

  • Fall damage still applies to launched players unless they have god mode.
  • A player in a vehicle is taken out of it first.
  • Things without physics cannot be launched; you get a notice.
  • Rank immunity applies: you cannot launch players ranked above you.
  • Each launch is written to the action log.
