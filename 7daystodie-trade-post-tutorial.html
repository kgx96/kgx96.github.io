07 GUIDES.

7 DAYS TO DIE MOD

KGX Trade Post.How to use it.

KGX Trade Post lets players trade with each other, even when they are offline. These guides cover buying, selling, the Collect box and the tools for server owners.

Tutorial 01

Build and open the Trade Post

Craft the Trade Post and find your way around.

  1. Craft the KGX Trade Post in your backpack: 8 forged iron, 2 mechanical parts and 10 paper. It looks like a mail drop box.
  2. Place it and press E.
  3. The tabs: Buy orders (what others want), For sale (what others sell), Order items (post your own buy order), Sell items (list your items), My trades and Collect.
  4. Hover over any button or row to read what it does.

Tutorial 02

Post a buy order

Ask for items and let other players deliver them.

  1. Open Order items, search for the item and press Pick.
  2. For an item with quality (weapons, tools, armour), set Min quality.
  3. Type the Amount and your Price each, then press Post order. The Dukes leave your backpack and are held for the order.
  4. If someone already lists the item at your price or less, you buy it at once.
  5. Delivered items wait in your Collect tab. The price guide line shows what the item usually goes for.

Tutorial 03

Sell your items

List items for other players to buy.

  1. Open Sell items and press Pick on a backpack stack.
  2. Type the Amount (empty = the whole stack) and your Price each, then press List items.
  3. Items with quality are listed one at a time, exactly as they are, with their mods and durability.
  4. If a buy order pays your price or more, it sells at once. Otherwise other players buy it from For sale, and you get the Dukes even when you are offline.

Tutorial 04

Buy from listings and deliver to buy orders

Trade with other players' offers.

  1. For sale: press Buy on a listing. An empty Amount buys as many as you can pay.
  2. Buy orders: press Deliver on an order for an item you carry. You are paid at once (minus the server's fee, if any).
  3. Use Show to filter by category and Sort to sort by price or name.

Tutorial 05

Manage your trades and the Collect box

Change prices, cancel and collect what you are owed.

  1. My trades: press Pick on an order or listing, type a new price and press Set price, or press Cancel order.
  2. Collect: bought items, unsold items and unspent Dukes wait here. Press Collect all.
  3. Orders end by themselves after 7 game days by default; what is left goes to your Collect box.
  4. When you join, a toast tells you what sold, what arrived and what ended while you were away.

Tutorial 06

Private offers, watching items and paying from the bank

The extra tools for traders.

  1. Only for: type a player's name when you post, and only that player sees your order or listing.
  2. Watch: in Order items, pick an item and press Watch. You get a toast when someone lists it or wants it (up to 20 items; kgx trade watch lists them).
  3. You also get a toast when someone posts a buy order for an item you carry. Turn toasts off with tradeAlerts in the KGX Settings window.
  4. With KGX Dukes Bank on the server, press Pay from: Backpack so it says Pay from: Bank to pay from your bank account.

Tutorial 07

Server owner: server orders, fees and markets

Run your server's economy.

  1. Server orders: kgx trade serverbuy resourceScrapIron 2 makes the server buy scrap iron at 2 Dukes each, for ever. kgx trade serversell foodCanSoup 30 10 sells 10 cans at 30 each. kgx trade cancel <id> removes one.
  2. The Trade Post Item Catalog lists every item's internal name and builds these commands for you.
  3. Blocked items: kgx trade block <item> keeps an item out; its open orders go back to the owners. kgx trade unblock <item> and kgx trade blocked.
  4. Settings (F2 or kgx config trade): feePercent (cut of each sale), postFee (Dukes per new order), expireDays, maxOrders, marketRange (local markets in blocks), alerts and tradeLog.
  5. Trade log: every trade is written to TradeLog.csv in the world's KGX\kgx.trade folder; kgx trade history <player> shows a player's last trades.

Tip: A server buy price above a server sell price for the same item is refused, so players cannot make Dukes from nothing.
