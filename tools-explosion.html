Explosion

Set off explosions with adjustable type, radius and damage.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6 / Updated Oct 26, 2026

What it does

Explosion sets off a blast where you aim, or centered on the player or entity you aim at. You pick the type, radius, center damage and push force in the tool panel.

Damage and push fall off in a straight line from full at the center to nothing at the edge of the radius. Everything in the radius can be hurt, pushed and (with the Fire type) set alight.

A fuse delay turns any blast into a timed charge. It beeps once per second at the blast point, and reload cancels it before it goes off.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Fun > Explosion.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Hurts only their own things (others with kgxat_grant_others).

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Explode on the ground where you aim
Right click Explode on the player/entity you aim at
Reload Cancel your pending fuses

While you hold the tool, a red sphere and a yellow ground ring show the radius at your crosshair. The HUD under the crosshair shows the type and, when set, the fuse time.

Options

Option What it does Default Range
Type The kind of blast (see below) Standard -
Radius Size of the blast area 256 16-2048
Damage (center) Damage at the center; less toward the edge 100 0-10000
Push force How hard things are thrown outward 600 0-5000

Screen shake Shakes the screens of nearby players On -
Scorch mark Leaves a scorch decal On -
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to the values above - -

The explosion types

Type What it does
Standard One explosion with damage and push
Big A large bomb effect plus four extra explosion effects around it, with a stronger screen shake
Fire Like Standard, and it sets players, NPCs and physics props alight for the burn time
Shockwave (push, no damage) A dust ring that pushes but never damages; no scorch mark
Cluster Several smaller blasts (one third of the radius each) at random ground points, 0.12 s apart
Visual only (no damage) Only the effect, a lighter shake and the scorch mark; no damage and no push

How to use it

Blow up a spot on the map
  1. Pick Type and set Radius and Damage (center).
  2. Aim at the ground or a wall and left-click.
Blow up a player or prop
  1. Aim at the player, NPC or prop.
  2. Right-click. The blast is centered on the target.
Plant a timed charge
  1. Set Fuse delay to, for example, 5.
  2. Left-click where the charge should go. It beeps each second, then explodes.
  3. To call it off, press reload. Every fuse you placed is cancelled.

Settings and commands

These caps limit what a limited player's Explosion can do. Set them in Settings > Limits (superadmins only).

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_cap_explode_radius Largest radius a limited player gets 512 0-2048
kgxat_cap_explode_damage Highest damage a limited player gets 150 0-10000

Limited players are players given the tool, and admins when kgxat_limits_admins is 1. Superadmins are never limited.

Tips and limits

  • Rank immunity applies. Players you cannot target are not hurt or pushed, and right-click refuses them.
  • The settings are read at the click, so a fuse uses the values you had when you placed it.
  • Your pending fuses are cancelled when you disconnect.
  • Viewmodels, held weapons and parented (attached) entities are never pushed or damaged.
  • Every blast is written to the action log with its type, radius and damage.
