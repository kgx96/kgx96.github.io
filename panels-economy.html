Economy

The live money overview: every player's wallet and bank, give/take/set money, and every store, bank and ATM on the map.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

On this page

  2. Where to find it

What it does

This is the Economy panel in the Economy category. It is the day-to-day view of the server's money. It is not the Economy page under Settings, which holds the server settings (currency, starting money, interest, cash).

The top list shows every player online with their wallet and bank. Right-click a player to give, take or set money. The bottom list shows every store, bank and ATM on this map, with quick actions to edit, visit, move or remove them.

A status line at the top tells you if the economy is on, the currency in use, or that DarkRP is running. With DarkRP, wallets are DarkRP money; banks, stores and ATMs still use the toolbox.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Economy > Economy.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can use it if a superadmin ticks Economy (money of every player, stores list) in Settings > Player Access. The Reset everyone's money button shows only for superadmins.

The panel

Players online
Item What it does
Player list Columns Player, Wallet and Bank. Click a column header to sort.
Right-click a player Wallet or Bank, then Give..., Take from... or Set to.... A box asks for the amount.
Saved accounts line How many saved accounts there are (players who have joined).
Refresh players Reloads the list.
Stores, banks and ATMs on this map
Item What it does
Store list Columns Name, Type, Items, Access and Owner.
Double-click a store Teleports you to it.
Right-click > Edit... Opens the store editor (the same one as Store Maker right-click).
Right-click > Go there Teleports you to it.
Right-click > Move it to where I am looking Moves the store to your aim point, facing you. The new spot is saved.
Right-click > Remove Deletes the store, its items and settings (asks first).
Refresh stores Reloads the list.
Superadmins
Item What it does
Reset everyone's money (all wallets and banks)... Sets every wallet and bank to 0, including offline players. It asks first and cannot be undone.

How to use it

Set a player's bank to an exact amount
  1. Find the player in the top list.
  2. Right-click them and choose Bank > Set to....
  3. Type the amount and press OK. The list updates.
Find and fix a store
  1. Find the store in the bottom list.
  2. Double-click it to go there, or right-click > Edit... to change it from where you are.
  3. To move it, look at the new spot, then right-click > Move it to where I am looking.
Wipe the economy for a new season
  1. Log in as a superadmin.
  2. Click Reset everyone's money (all wallets and banks)....
  3. Confirm with Reset all. Every saved account is cleared and players online drop to 0.

Tips and limits

  • Give and take are logged in the Action Log. Take never goes below 0.
  • You can only change money of players you are allowed to target (not higher ranks).
  • Player Manager > Money gives, takes or sets money too; it needs access to this panel.
  • Store actions work on stores you may edit: admins can edit every store, other players only their own.
  • After a reset, players who were online stay at 0. Offline players have no saved account any more, so they get the starting money again when they next join.
  • Make stores with Economy > Store Maker. Give money with a click using Economy > Money.
  • Server money settings are in Settings > Economy; paychecks in Settings > Paychecks.
