Buy Stations

Wall weapons, a mystery box, perk machines, door buys and switches for Waves (PVE) matches.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

Buy Stations (v3.6.17) puts things on the map that players in a Waves match buy with their points, like the wall buys of a zombies mode. Players earn points for every hit and kill on the enemies; everyone starts with the mode's starting points.

A player presses E on a station to buy. The station shows its name and price over it (a wall weapon also shows the weapon floating in front of it). Outside a Waves match a station shows "Waves matches only" and sells nothing.

Stations are saved per map (data/kgx_admin_toolbox/buystations_<map>.json), come back after a map cleanup, and are deleted by a map wipe (Settings > Reset everything / wipe map data...).

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Buy Stations.

Admins only: stations hand out weapons and perks, so only toolbox admins can place or remove them. The tool cannot be given to players; it is greyed out in Settings > Player Access.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place a station. On a wall a wall weapon lies flat against it; the other kinds stand on the floor in front of the wall. On the floor it faces you.
Right click Remove the station you aim at, or the nearest one within 96 units.

The kinds

Kind What the player gets Default price
Wall weapon The weapon. Buying it again gives ammo for half the price. A player holds at most two bought weapons; a third replaces the one in their hand. 500
Mystery box A random weapon from the box's list that the player does not hold yet (default: AR2, Shotgun, .357, Crossbow, RPG, SMG, Grenade, SLAM); holding them all, ammo for one. 950
Perk machine An Armory perk (Settings > Armory) or a buff (World > Create Buffs) until the player goes down. Perks held already do not give their health, armor or buff again. 2500
Door buy The barrier opens for everyone until the match ends. 750
Switch Fires a logic node (a power switch, a trap, lights). 0

Every kind can also fire a logic node when bought.

The panel

Option What it does Default
Kind Which kind of station the next click places. Wall weapon
Price in points (0 = the kind's default) 0-20000. 0
Shown name (empty = the weapon's / perk's) The name over the station. empty
Weapon class Wall weapon only. Type a class or search the list below it and click a weapon. weapon_ar2
Perk Perk machine only. The Armory perks and every buff. Vitality
Box weapons (comma separated; empty = HL2 weapons) Mystery box only. Up to 30 classes. empty
Model Any kind. Empty uses the kind's model: a plate, an ammo crate, a vending machine, a concrete barrier, a button. empty
Fire a logic node when bought Any kind. A node id like L3 or L3:Input (World > Logic). empty

How to use it

Set up a Survival map
  1. Place Enemy spawn spots with World > Mode Spots (For the mode: Survival). Put them away from where players start.
  2. Take Buy Stations. Set Kind to Wall weapon, pick the Shotgun in the list, set the price to 750 and left-click a wall near the start.
  3. Set Kind to Door buy and place it in a doorway to a second room. Players open it for 750 points when they want more room.
  4. Set Kind to Perk machine, pick Vitality and place it in the second room.
  5. Set Kind to Mystery box and place it somewhere hard to reach.
  6. Start Survival in Utilities > Game Modes.
A power switch
  1. In World > Logic, make the logic that opens a map door or turns on lights, and note its node id (for example L4).
  2. Place a Switch with Fire a logic node when bought set to L4 and the price you want (0 is free).

Tips

  • Stations close again (barriers come back) when the match ends or a new lobby opens.
  • Points are the match money of the Waves mode; they are not the server money and end with the match.
  • Keep two bought weapons in mind when you price walls: players swap, they do not collect. A weapon is only taken away when the new one was given.
  • The name over a wall weapon is the weapon's name in each player's language.
