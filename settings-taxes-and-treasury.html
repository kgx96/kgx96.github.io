Taxes & Treasury

Income tax on paychecks, sales tax on store and shipment prices, transfer fees and property tax, collected into a server treasury that admins and Treasury jobs (a Mayor) pay players out of.

What it does

Taxes & Treasury (v3.6.20) takes money out of the economy in four ways and keeps it in one server account, the treasury:

  • Income tax: a percent of every toolbox paycheck.
  • Sales tax: a percent added on top of store and shipment prices.
  • Transfer fee: a percent (with a minimum) of money given with !give or sent by bank transfer; the sender pays it.
  • Property tax: a percent of a bought property's price, charged every tax period (counted for each property from when it was bought).

Everything is off (0) by default. With all taxes at 0, nothing changes on your server.

The treasury is spent by admins and by jobs with the Treasury ability (for example a Mayor): they pay players out of it with a reason (a bonus, a reward, a job well done). Every tax, fee and payment is in Utilities > Money Log as its own source, and the treasury shows there as the player Treasury.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Settings > Taxes & Treasury. Only superadmins change the settings.

The treasury window: !treasury in chat, kgxat_treasury_open in the console, or Open the treasury on this page. Admins with the Economy panel and players whose job has the Treasury ability (Settings > Jobs) can open it.

The treasury log

The log keeps the last 1000 changes in treasury.json. Right-click a line or the empty part of the log:

Option Who What it does
Copy this line, Copy the amount, Copy the amount in words, Copy the detail, Copy the note Everyone who can open the window Copies to the clipboard.
Deposit..., Withdraw... Admins with the Economy panel Asks for the amount, shows it in words, then asks for a reason or note.
Add a note line... Superadmins A line of its own with your note (up to 120 letters), for example "Server grant for the police budget".
Add a note to this line... / Edit the note... Superadmins A note on any line. Empty removes the note; an empty note on a note line removes that line.
Take this deposit back... Superadmins On an admin's deposit: takes that amount back out of the treasury (never more than it has) as a new line, and marks the deposit "(taken back)". Only once per deposit.
Remove this line... Superadmins Removes the line from the log. The balance does not change.
Clear the log... Superadmins Empties the log and In today; one line says who cleared it. The balance does not change.

Removing lines and clearing the log never touch the Money Log: Utilities > Money Log keeps every change (and rollbacks need it). Every superadmin change is in the Action log.

Amounts in words

Every amount box shows its amount in words, like on a check, always with the cents (v3.6.21): 1000000 is One Million Dollars and 00/100, 12.50 is Twelve Dollars and 50/100. With another currency it uses its name: "Twelve Euros and 50/100", "Twelve Credits and 50/100". The words are English only.

How to use it

A Mayor who pays bonuses
  1. Settings > Jobs: edit the Mayor job and tick the Treasury ability. Save.
  2. Settings > Taxes & Treasury: set Income tax to 10 and Sales tax to 5. Click Apply the numbers above.
  3. The Mayor types !treasury, picks a player, types 200 and "Kept the streets clean", and presses Pay from the treasury.
A money sink against inflation
  1. Untick Keep taxes and fees in the treasury.
  2. Set a Transfer fee of 2% and a Property tax of 0.5%.
  3. Money taken this way leaves the economy. Utilities > Money Log shows each charge on the player.
Note a big deposit
  1. As an admin, type 1000000 in the deposit box: "One Million Dollars" shows under it.
  2. Type "Server grant" as the reason and press Deposit.
  3. As a superadmin, right-click the new line > Add a note to this line... and write what it is for.

Tips and limits

  • Income tax only touches toolbox paychecks; with DarkRP, DarkRP's own salaries are not taxed. DarkRP's own money transfers and doors are not taxed either.
  • Taxes the toolbox takes are rounded down, but a sales tax is at least 1 on a paid item. With cents on (Settings > Economy), taxes are kept to the nearest cent and a sales tax is at least $0.01.
  • Property tax is not taken? Open the treasury and read the line under the rates: it says why. The first tax of a property comes one period after it was bought; Charge property tax now charges at once to test it.
  • Every payment and every tax can be undone in Utilities > Money Log (Rollbacks).
  • Players see the tax lines in their own language (Settings > Display > Language).
