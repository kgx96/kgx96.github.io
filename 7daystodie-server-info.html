v1.0.0
Out now/ 01

First release

A welcome window with your server rules, which players accept as they join.

  • Welcome window on a player's first join (or every join), with {server} and {player} filled in; Home opens it any time
  • Up to 15 numbered rules with Accept and Decline, and four modes: show until accepted, freeze until accepted (no moving, no weapons, no damage), kick on decline (with a time limit) or information only
  • Everyone accepts again when the rules change; admins and the host are never kicked, and only asked to accept (or frozen) when you turn that on
  • Message of the day as a toast after every join; up to 10 announcements repeating every 5 to 1440 minutes; kgx info announce for one right now
  • Server page: world, day, day length, blood moon, loot, XP, players, PvP, land claims and the KGX mods installed, plus your own lines with a Copy links button
  • Edit tab for admins to change every text in game, or edit serverinfo.txt by hand and reload; kgx info status and kgx info reset

Next update, in progress/ 02

  • Kick on decline and the accept time limit tested on a dedicated server

  • Fixes for what players report on Nexus Mods for v1.0.0

02 / About the project

Made for
7 Days to Die servers.

KGX Server Info

Players who join your server see your welcome text and your rules, and accept them. Choose how strict it is: show the rules on every join until they are accepted, freeze players who have not accepted, disconnect players who decline, or show them for information only. Add a message of the day, announcements that repeat on a timer and a server page with your Discord, website and wipe date. Admins write everything in the window's Edit tab.

KGX Server Info on Nexus Mods

Getting started

Needs KGX Core (7DTD) 1.1.0 or newer and 7 Days to Die V 3.4 (b10) on PC with EasyAntiCheat off. Extract the zip into the game's Mods folder so you have Mods\KGX_ServerInfo next to Mods\0_KGX_Core; install both on the server and on every player's game. As an admin, press Home in game and open the Edit tab to write your welcome text and rules. Type kgx info help in the console (F1) for the commands.
