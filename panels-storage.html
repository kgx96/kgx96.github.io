Storage

List every storage and loot container on the map to edit, visit, refill, empty, move or remove it.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 27, 2026

What it does

The Storage panel lists every container on the current map. From here you can open a container's editor, go to it, refill loot, empty it, move it or remove it.

You make containers with World > Create Storage. This panel manages the ones that exist. Loot containers fill from loot tables, which you set up in World > Loot Tables.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > World > Storage.

Toolbox admins can use it. Superadmins can also give it to players in Settings > Player Access with the Storage (containers list, loot tables) tick. Players who have the Create Storage tool see their own containers in this list.

The panel

Container list
Column What it shows
Name The container's name.
Kind storage or loot.
Items Items in it and its slots, for example 3/8.
Owner Who made it: their name when online, else their SteamID.

Double-click a row to go to the container. Right-click a row for:

Option What it does
Edit... Opens the container editor (the same as right-clicking it with Create Storage).
Go there Teleports you in front of it, facing it.
Refill now (loot) Rolls fresh loot. Only loot containers refill.
Empty it Removes everything in it, including each player's own loot.
Move it to where I am looking Moves it to your crosshair, facing you.
Remove Removes the container and everything in it, after a confirmation.
Buttons
Button What it does
Refresh list Reloads the list.
Refill every loot container now Refills every loot container on the map.

How to use it

Find and edit a container
  1. Open the panel and find the container by Name.
  2. Right-click it and choose Edit....
  3. Change its settings in the editor and click Save.
Reset all loot before an event
  1. Click Refill every loot container now.
  2. Every loot container rolls new loot from its table.
Clear out a storage container
  1. Right-click the container and choose Empty it.
  2. To remove it entirely, choose Remove and confirm.
Move a container
  1. Look at the spot where it should go.
  2. Right-click the container in the list and choose Move it to where I am looking.

Tips and limits

  • Containers are saved per map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/storage_<map>.json.
  • Dropped backpack bags are not listed.
  • Players with only the Storage panel tick cannot edit, empty or remove containers they do not own. Toolbox admins can change any container.
  • Refill now (loot) on a storage container shows "Only loot containers refill."
  • Removing a container cannot be undone; its contents are lost.
