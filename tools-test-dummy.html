Test Dummy

Spawn a test dummy that counts as a player: T-pose or A-pose, follow, wander, take damage with damage numbers, die, respawn, explode.

What it does

A test dummy is a bot player. Every toolbox tool, the Player Manager, your admin mod and the gamemode treat it like any player. Use it to try out jails, money, jobs, weapons and other features without a second person.

A dummy can stand in a T-pose or A-pose, move around, take damage and show floating damage numbers. It can die, explode, and respawn after a delay. A label above it shows its name, health, armor and total damage taken.

Each dummy takes a player slot. It does not work in single player; you need a listen or dedicated server with free slots.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Test Dummy.

Admins only by default. A superadmin can give it to all players or to one rank in Settings > Player Access, where the tick notes that it uses real player slots. Players given the tool can only control their own dummies and are limited by kgxat_limit_dummies.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Their own dummy, no weapon, not invincible (real player slot).

The dummy list

Below the options is a list of the test dummies on the server, with Dummy, HP and Damage columns. Double-click a row to go to that dummy. Right-click a row for the same action menu as Shift + right-click.

The action menu has:

  • Movement: Follow me, Stop (stand still), Wander around here, Strafe left and right, Run in circles.
  • Poses: T-pose, A-pose, Normal animations.
  • Health: Heal, Invincible on/off, Reset damage counter, Kill, Explode, Respawn.
  • Place: Bring here (new home spot), Go to, Respawn here from now on.
  • Tests: Press E on what I aim at (as the dummy), Rob what I aim at (money source, as the dummy), Try to break free of the handcuffs, Send me a mail, Put its Storage Vault in front of me, Go on air (a screen and a radio in front of me).
  • Act like a cheater (anti-cheat test): speed hack, fly hack, teleport, noclip, aim snaps, or Play fair again.
  • Try to take a job (as a player), when jobs are on.
  • Remove.

Remove all my test dummies asks for confirmation, then removes them.

How to use it

Set up a target for aim practice
  1. Set Movement to Strafe left and right (aim practice) and Pose to Normal (animated).
  2. Tick Invincible (gets hit, never loses health) if you want it to keep standing.
  3. Left-click the ground. "Dummy 1" appears facing you.
  4. Shoot it. Red numbers are damage, yellow are headshots, grey means it is invincible.
Test a tool on a player
  1. Left-click to place a dummy.
  2. Use the tool on it, or open the Player Manager; the dummy is listed there like any player.
Change a dummy that is already placed
  1. Change the options in the panel.
  2. Aim at the dummy and right-click. You see "Updated Dummy 1." Its name does not change.
Test a job or a store as a player
  1. Shift + right-click the dummy, or right-click it in the list.
  2. Pick Try to take a job (as a player) or Press E on what I aim at (as the dummy).
  3. The dummy's messages come to you, starting with its name, such as "[Dummy 1]".

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_limit_dummies Test dummies one limited player may have (Settings > Limits). 0 = not allowed. 1 0-16

Tips and limits

  • Go to, Press E on what I aim at, Rob what I aim at, Put its Storage Vault in front of me, Go on air and Act like a cheater are for admins only. Players given the tool can arm a dummy only with spawnable weapons that are not admin-only.
  • When the server is full, spawning fails with "No free player slot".
  • Removing a dummy kicks the bot with "Test dummy removed".
  • Wander around here, Strafe left and right and Run in circles in the action menu use the dummy's current spot as its new home.
  • Follow me in the panel follows the player who spawned it. In the action menu it follows whoever picked it.
  • An explosion from Explode or Explode when it dies only hurts the dummy.
  • For an admin, Remove all my test dummies removes every test dummy on the server. E + reload removes only your own.
