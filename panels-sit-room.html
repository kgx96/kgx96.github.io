Sit Room

Take players aside to talk in a sit room, then send everyone back to where they were.

Panel / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

A sit takes a player aside so you can talk to them. Starting a sit moves you and the player to a sit room. Ending it sends everyone back to where they stood before.

Without a sit room on the map, the player is brought to you instead. Several sit rooms let several admins hold sits at the same time.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Utilities > Sit Room.

Toolbox admins can use it. Players can get it through Settings > Player Access with the Sit Room tick. Without access, the panel says "Only admins (or players given access) can use sit rooms."

Other ways in:

  • Chat: !sit <name> and !endsit.
  • Player Manager: Start a sit / bring into my sit, Send back from my sit and End my sit.
  • Reports: right-click a report > Start a sit with the reporter.

The panel

Sits running now
Item What it does
Sit list Columns Admin, Where and Players. Your own sit shows "(you)".
End my sit (send everyone back) Ends your sit and returns everyone in it.
Player Pick another human player. Bots are not listed.
Bring them into my sit Adds the picked player to your sit, or starts one.
Send them back from my sit Returns the picked player to where they were.
Sit rooms on this map
Item What it does
Room list Columns # and Name.
Right-click > Go there Teleports you to the room.
Right-click > Remove Removes the room.
Name (optional) Name for a new room. Empty gives "Sit room N".
Add a sit room where I stand Saves your position and view direction as a sit room.
Refresh Reloads the player list, sits and rooms.

How to use it

Set up a sit room
  1. Go to a quiet, closed-off spot on the map.
  2. Open Utilities > Sit Room, type a name and press Add a sit room where I stand.
  3. Add more rooms if several admins hold sits at once.
Hold a sit
  1. Type !sit <name> in chat, or pick the player and press Bring them into my sit.
  2. You and the player are moved to a free sit room. The player sees "An admin (your name) would like to talk to you."
  3. Add more players the same way if needed.
  4. Type !endsit or press End my sit (send everyone back). Everyone returns to where they were.

Settings and commands

Command What it does
!sit <name> Starts a sit with the player, or adds them to yours. Without a name, uses the player you aim at.
!endsit Ends your sit and sends everyone back.

Tips and limits

  • At most 10 sit rooms per map. Rooms are saved per map in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/sitrooms_<map>.json.
  • A jailed player cannot be added. Release them first.
  • A player can only be in one admin's sit at a time.
  • Dead players are respawned when they are brought in.
  • Players who are dead or jailed when the sit ends stay where they are.
  • If the admin leaves the server, their sit ends and everyone is sent back.
  • Players in a sit show "in a sit" in the Player Manager status.
  • The rank immunity check applies when you add a player.
