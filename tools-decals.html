Decals

Blood, scorch marks, paint splats, graffiti and any decal material; size and color.

What it does

Decals sprays a decal where you aim. You can use a game decal (Blood, Scorch, paint splats, impacts and more) or any material under materials/decals, such as graffiti, cracks or signs.

Decal materials take the size and color you set. Game decals keep their own look. You can scatter several decals with one click, or hold the mouse button to spray a stream.

Every player sees the decals. Admins can keep them for the map, so they come back after a cleanup and show for players who join later.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Properties > Decals.

Admin only by default. Superadmins can give it to players or ranks in Settings > Player Access. Players given the tool can only place decals while kgxat_decals_players is on.

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): On the world and their own things; not kept.

Controls

Key What it does
Left click Place the decal (hold for a stream when "Hold to spray" is on)
Right click Pick a decal
Reload Admins: remove kept decals near the aim (everyone's decals are redrawn)

The HUD under the crosshair shows the current decal.

Server settings in the panel

Admins see three server settings in the tool panel. Only superadmins can change them. The checkbox applies at once; the sliders need Apply.

Option Setting Default Range
Players given this tool may place decals kgxat_decals_players 1 0-1
Players: decals per second kgxat_decals_rate 4 1-30
Most decals kept for one map kgxat_decals_kept_max 500 10-3000

The decal picker

Right-click, or click Pick a decal..., to open the Pick a decal window. Game decals are listed first, then the materials found under materials/decals, grouped by folder in the Kind column.

Type in the search box (blood, scorch, paint, graffiti...) to filter the list. Selecting a material shows a preview. Double-click a row, or select it and click Use selected (or double-click).

How to use it

Splatter blood
  1. Right-click and pick Blood.
  2. Set How many at once to 5 and Scatter spread to 64.
  3. Aim at a wall or floor and left-click.
Spray colored graffiti
  1. Right-click, search for a graffiti material and double-click it.
  2. Pick a color and set Width and Height, for example 3.
  3. Tick Hold to spray if you want a stream, then left-click.
Decorate the map for good (admins)
  1. Tick Keep for this map.
  2. Place your decals.
  3. They come back after a map cleanup and appear for players who join.
Remove kept decals
  1. Aim near the decals you want gone and press reload.
  2. Kept decals near the aim point are deleted. Everyone's decals are cleared and the remaining kept ones are redrawn.

Settings and commands

Command What it does
kgxat_decals_clear Clears decals for everyone; kept ones are drawn again (admins)
kgxat_decals_clear all Clears every decal and forgets the kept ones too (admins)

Kept decals are saved in data/kgx_admin_toolbox/decals_<map>.json.

Tips and limits

  • Players are limited to 5 decals per click and a size of 4. Their decals are never kept.
  • Admins can place up to 30 per click with a size up to 16.
  • While holding to spray, admins place a decal every 0.05 s and players every 0.25 s.
  • When the kept list is full, the oldest decals are dropped first.
  • Decals disappear for a player who clears decals on their side (r_cleardecals).
