Paint Brush

Hold the mouse to paint color and/or material onto props under the brush.

Tool / Covers v3.6.21.6.1 / Updated Oct 28, 2026

What it does

The Paint Brush colors props in an area instead of one at a time. Hold left mouse and sweep the brush over a build. Every prop and vehicle inside the brush takes the color, the material, or both.

A red sphere and a ground ring at your crosshair show the brush. Its size matches the Brush size slider. Right-click copies the look of a prop, and reload cleans props back to plain white with their normal material.

The brush paints entities whose class starts with prop_ and vehicles. It never paints players.

Where to find it

Q menu > KGX's Admin Toolbox > Properties > Paint Brush.

Admins only by default. A superadmin can give it to all players or to one rank in Settings > Player Access, or add it to a Tool License (Settings > Licenses).

Given to a player (v3.6.11.3): Paints their own props only; menu materials only, never fully invisible.

Controls

Key What it does
Left (hold) Paint the props under the brush. Painting repeats about 10 times a second while you hold the button.
Right Pick up the color and material of the aimed prop into the panel.
Reload Clean the props under the brush: white, normal material.

Options

Option What it does Default
Paint Color only, Material only, or Color and material. Decides what a stroke changes. Color only
Brush size Radius of the brush, 8 to 1024 units. 64
Color Color picker with an alpha bar. Alpha below 255 makes props see-through. 255, 60, 60, 255
Material (empty = normal) Material path to paint. Leave it empty to paint the normal material. empty
Material list Thumbnails of the standard override materials. Click one to fill the material box.
Reset to defaults Puts every option back to its default.

How to use it

Paint a build one color
  1. Set Paint to Color only.
  2. Pick a color in Color.
  3. Set Brush size so the sphere covers a few props.
  4. Hold left mouse and sweep the sphere across the build.
Match the look of an existing prop
  1. Set Paint to Color and material.
  2. Aim at the prop you want to copy and right-click. You see "Picked up the look of ...".
  3. Hold left mouse over the props that should match.
Undo paint on an area
  1. Aim at the painted props.
  2. Press reload. Every prop under the brush turns white with its normal material.

Settings and commands

Setting What it does Default Range
kgxat_cap_area_radius Largest brush a limited player can use (Settings > Limits). Admins are not capped unless kgxat_limits_admins is on; superadmins never are. 512 0-16384

Tips and limits

  • With built-in prop protection on (kgxat_pp), non-admin players only paint their own props and props of players who made them a buddy. Admins paint anything.
  • Reload cleans to plain white. It does not restore a color the prop had before you painted it.
  • The tool does not save paint. A permanent entity (World > Permanent Entities) keeps the color and material stored in its permanent entry.
  • For paint on walls and floors, use Properties > Spray Paint instead.
