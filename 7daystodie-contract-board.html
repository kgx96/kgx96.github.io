v1.0.1
Out now/ 01

Works with KGX Dukes Bank

A Contract Board you build yourself, with daily contracts for Dukes, XP and items. Version 1.0.1 can pay contract Dukes into KGX Dukes Bank.

  • Craft the Contract Board (10 wood, 10 nails, 5 paper) and press E: every player gets their own board with 5 offers and new offers every game day
  • Kill quotas (zombies, ferals, radiated, zombie dogs, cops, spiders), deliveries (wood, scrap iron, feathers, raw meat, cloth, bandages, oil, electrical parts), survive the blood moon or stay alive 12 to 48 hours, clear POIs of 1+ to 5+ skulls
  • Tiers from player level: harder contracts and 10% more reward per tier; up to 3 contracts at once
  • Party sharing: kills count for party members nearby; a POI clear counts for every player with a matching contract who was inside
  • Your contracts on the HUD tracker; mouse tooltips on every contract and button
  • Server settings (offers, contracts at once, refresh days, reward scale, POI clear %) and contracts.json for your own list; contracts that need an item or creature another mod removed are left out
  • New in 1.0.1: with KGX Dukes Bank installed, choose “Contract Dukes: into the bank” at a KGX ATM and contract Dukes go straight into your bank account (optional; Contract Board works without it)

02 / About the project

Made for
7 Days to Die.

KGX Contract Board

A Contract Board you build yourself. Press E and pick from five contracts a day: kill quotas, deliveries, surviving the blood moon or clearing a POI of a set skull level. Finish it, come back, turn it in and get paid in Dukes, XP and items. Every player has their own board; party members nearby share kill progress, and a POI clear counts for everyone on the contract who was inside. With KGX Dukes Bank installed, contract Dukes can go straight into your bank account.

KGX Contract Board on Nexus Mods

Getting started

Needs KGX Core (7DTD) 1.1.0 or newer and 7 Days to Die V 3.4 (b10) on PC with EasyAntiCheat off. Extract the zip into the game's Mods folder so you have Mods\KGX_ContractBoard next to Mods\0_KGX_Core; in multiplayer install both on the server and on every player's game. Craft the Contract Board in your backpack (10 wood, 10 nails, 5 paper), place it and press E. Optional: add KGX Dukes Bank to have contract Dukes paid into your bank account.
